# Numinous 0.5.0 alpha 2

Rooms stay readable when the window gets small, and exported collections keep
all their rooms.

## Readable rooms and complete pictures

Room titles now fit beside Journey progress. Audio status has its own row,
and spectrum bars stay inside the header instead of erasing mathematical
marks. Long Show title cards fit their viewport. Composition regressions
cover every catalog title at compact size and explanations at medium widths.

Percolation's shortest crossing now has a distinct character in the terminal
and MCP picture, with brighter ink in pixel renders. An independent exhaustive
small-grid comparison verifies that its compact search state preserves the
shortest answer.

Contact sheets resize their tiles to include the entire catalog within the
image bound. Captions stay inside their own tiles, and the command reports
the dimensions it actually wrote.

## Bounded creation handling

Share exports reject invalid size and phase before creating output folders
or files. Gallery discovery retains only the newest displayable creations
as it scans, with stable timestamp ties and preserved remix lineage, rather
than loading the entire archive before applying its display cap. Existing
bounded file readers and link exclusions remain in place.

The previous corrections remain: unknown bare Studio names are refused,
Mandelbrot keeps its multicolor detail and quiet ground, and oversized
optional MCP audio keeps its notes and Show continuation.

## An active milestone

Sensory Alpha remains active. General Cabinet and HUD scaling, the closed
physical display pacing set, and remaining photosensitivity decisions stay
open. Native Overture playback and Vibe follow in roadmap order. Human
playtests are optional feedback and do not gate this release.

To compare this round, resize an App room and inspect its title, progress,
audio status, and explanation. Try dense Percolation in the terminal and
export a contact sheet with a few columns. The machine regressions cover
these cases without requiring a human playtest before the next change.
