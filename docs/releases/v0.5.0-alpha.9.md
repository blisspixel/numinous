# Numinous 0.5.0 alpha 9

`tour`, `watch`, and `play` take a live terminal when stdout is a terminal.
Each frame is one synchronized update. A pipe or a file gets one still
postcard and the title, then the command exits, and it does not light a star.

Grouped help wraps to the terminal, or to `COLUMNS` when stdout is not a
terminal. 60 columns is a normal width. `TERM=dumb` draws an ASCII picture.
`NO_COLOR` on a normal terminal keeps the block picture and drops the color.

The Cabinet, the reader, and banners share one chrome palette and do not
borrow the room's accent. The window stays dark. A light color table is
checked for contrast and is not a setting. A Visual Era change rides the
room dip. A new journey star lights once.

The spectral ink for `@` is teal. The Drifter is bright blue. New room
accents cannot enter the magenta band. Accents already in the catalog stay
until a shrink-only move can repaint them with the Sensory Lift.

Attention is a temperature hand and a placed key. One head, no learned
projections, and no values matrix. A packaged round called the room dull and
kept it. Strange Loop is a descent that returns to the frame it left, turned,
by the map in de Smit and Lenstra, Notices of the AMS 50(4), 2003. A packaged
round called that room worth staying in. Both rounds are recorded in
`docs/PLAYTESTS.md`. They are formative evidence.

Numinous has no companion glyph. γ stays a Constant in the lore. The
interface rules live in `docs/DESIGN.md`.

Listen is designed and not built. A preset would draw the whole frame from
the mix, in the manner of a visualization plugin. The meter that ships, and
Vibe, stay as they are.

Sensory Alpha remains active. Physical pacing receipts, the flash and
color-vision rulings, catalog-wide perceptual comparison, and further glow
stay open. This release does not claim the Sensory Lift is promoted.
Understanding Alpha's qualifying study remains open. Roadmap row 5 stays
open: the two rooms are rebuilt, and the thin-room scorecard and the
commissioned cohort are not.
