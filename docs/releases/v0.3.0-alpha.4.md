# Numinous 0.3.0 alpha 4

This prerelease gives Nontransitive Dice the seventh room-owned Universal
Wager and completes the prescribed depth wave.

## What changed

- Choose die A, B, or C first in the App or through MCP `die_choice`.
- Call which die can beat it through the bottom band, keys, or MCP
  `counter_wager`.
- Watch all 36 equally likely face pairs arrive as distinct W and L cells
  before the answer is named.
- Meet the exact counter, probability, and a graded sentence after
  consolidation.
- Observe without predicting by completing four die choices.

The exact cycle is A over B in 24 of 36 outcomes, B over C in 24 of 36, and C
over A in 20 of 36. The room's sampled rolls remain playable, but complete
enumeration owns the wager's truth. The experience turns nontransitivity into
a strategic reversal: no die is best independent of its opponent, and choosing
first lets the other player choose a counter.

## Agent experience

The MCP server advertises typed `die_choice` and `counter_wager` actions during
discovery. Its structured result includes all face arrays, the exact cycle,
chosen die, counter, wager, correctness, beat, earn, reveal gate, grading, and
visual outcome grid. A digital player never needs to infer coordinates to
participate. The local agent hallway exercises a cold open, a fertile miss, and
a correct consolidated call through real MCP stdio for five fixed lenses. It
uses no paid inference.
