# Numinous 0.4.0 alpha 11

The trick you finished is now a trick you can see.

A ninth source-blind playtest of the published `0.4.0-alpha.10` binaries
confirmed every claim that release made and then found the sharpest thing anyone
has said about The 720 Degree Room: it could now be finished, and finishing it
looked exactly like never having played. This release answers that, corrects a
second room that was counting the wrong thing, and removes two stale claims. It
also records three plans that were previously only intentions.

## The belt keeps the twist you took off

A winning poke returned `TURNS 2.00  OVER  FLAT`, `goalMet: true`, and `Touch: 0
of 2304 cells answered`, with the picture identical to the untouched room.

Nothing was wrong. Two turns come off and leave a flat belt; a flat belt is what
the room starts as; the stone's mark returns after every full turn, so at two
turns it points exactly where it pointed at rest. The finished trick is, frame
for frame, the picture of nobody having played. That is the entire point of the
room and it cannot be seen from the endpoint alone.

So the room keeps the twist that came off. Once the belt has been carried over
the stone, the frame draws it twice: the braid that was there before the pass,
in a fainter mark, and the belt that hangs now. From two turns that is a double
braid standing behind two flat lines. From one turn the two lie exactly on top
of each other, because a pass reverses a single twist into one that looks the
same, and the picture says the belt did not change.

That contrast is the double cover, drawn rather than told. It arrives without
the word Dirac and without the "two turns then lift" hint that two rounds have
now refused. The same winning poke answers 132 of 2304 cells.

## A room whose verb is TAKE A CELL now reports the cells

The Only Move answered every single click with `RULES 30  PLAYED 0  WON 0  TIED
0`. The click had taken a cell and the machine had answered it, and the picture
showed both, but the status counted finished games, and a row of zeros after an
action reads as nothing happening. The tester clicked twenty five times at
twenty five points before concluding that clicking does not play.

A game in play now reports the board it is in, `YOURS 1  MINE 1  OPEN 7`, and
the record returns when a game ends.

## Two claims that were no longer true

The README still promised sound returned as sound rather than as notation
describing it, two rounds after that was corrected in `PLAY.md` and in both
audio tool replies. It survived in the one file a reader who never opens
`PLAY.md` reads. It now says what they say: the file is sent beside the
notation, and whether it arrives as sound is the client's answer to give.

The 720 doorway still named Dirac's belt trick and told a player to keep turning
and count what it takes to undo, which is the refused hint in gentler words.
Both are gone. The reveal still names Dirac, SO(3), and Spin(3) once the trick
is done, and `dirac` and `double cover` join the enumerated answers no doorway
or unplayed status may print, so a guard holds this rather than a habit.

## Four connection edges

Rooms that already earned their explanation now hand over the room they rhyme
with. The Hopf Fibration names the Bloch Sphere, and the phase no measurement
can see is the fiber a player is flying. The Catenary and the Catenoid name each
other as least energy and least area with the same cosh. The Evolute and the
Cycloid share Huygens hanging a pendulum between cycloidal cheeks in 1673.
Stern-Brocot, Farey, and Calkin-Wilf name each other as three enumerations of
the rationals running on one mediant. Nine rooms gained or extended a cut, and
nothing moved into a doorway or a status line.

## Three plans, written down

**The Fair** is a designed, unbuilt room: a row of fairground stalls, not one of
them fair, each pricing itself on a balance beam where a weight's moment is its
contribution to the expectation. Seven stalls fix every term where a hand cannot
reach them, proved by machine rather than asserted in prose, and the eighth is a
roll-down whose threshold is a ratio rather than a prize size, so a buyer large
enough to matter pushes the price back under the line they crossed.

**A public house** records hosting Numinous where any mind can reach it without
installing anything, and opening a channel where the players propose the rooms,
with the four gates that decide whether it can be done well rather than merely
done.

**The other direction** extends `ROSETTA.md` from how a visitor gets in to what
this artifact would be worth to a finder who has no human language. The research
bounds the claim rather than flattering it: mathematics is a source of cribs and
not a substitute for a language, and the first step is worth building on its own
merits, which is that a room should state the mathematics it renders and a test
should recompute it.

## Boundaries this release keeps

Audio is still a sound sent rather than a sound heard, and the ninth round
confirmed both halves of that sentence again on a host with no sound device.
Stateless `play_room` still accepts a second named wager as a new first wager.
Three doors are quieter than 355 ids and are still not a single invitation, and
a room id that is not on the greeting still has to be guessed. Understanding
Alpha's exit remains open. Rounds and boundaries are in `docs/PLAYTESTS.md`;
gates are in `VERIFY.md`.
