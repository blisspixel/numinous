# Numinous 0.4.0 alpha 34

Read at your own size, without losing your place.

## A reading size that stays with you

Settings > Reading Text selects 100, 125, or 150 percent of the study reader's
responsive body size, using Left/Right on that Settings row. A-/A+ buttons and
minus/plus keys change it while reading;
equals also enlarges. Prose, equations, and references reflow around the source
position at each depth. Navigation stays fixed, and every depth remains freely
available. Size survives room and language changes and the next launch.

Older preferences keep their language, mute, window, era, and audio levels,
starting at 100 percent. Brackets still change volume while the reader is open.
Changing reading size leaves room tuning, phase, accepted inputs, Journey, and
audio playback intact. General Cabinet and HUD text scaling remains open.

Desktop Cabinet lists now page around the selected row when the whole list
cannot fit at its minimum readable size. Settings rows stay above the footer,
and keyboard and controller navigation can reach every row.
