# Numinous 0.5.0 alpha 1

Sensory Alpha begins with light that preserves what the rooms draw.

## Distinct marks across the rooms

The shared raster now separates faint guides, secondary detail, main shapes,
and hot highlights. Every catalog and hidden-room accent stays within a
validated lightness band and keeps graphical contrast against the near-black
stage. The terminal retains its original characters; the pixel face now
preserves their different weights. Mandelbrot keeps its continuous multicolor
field and quiet ground.

Hilbert lights a neighborhood along the thread. Percolation leaves closed
sites dark, lights the connected cluster, and exposes a shortest crossing.
Wireworld separates conductor, electron head, and tail. Visible pixel touch
responses across the catalog survive the color-free renderer. App footer and
audio labels retain readable main-idea ink.

Rule 30 now grows its named rule, with variations moving the initial seed.
The player's dial still chooses other rule bytes. Its former automatic rule
gallery mislabeled the picture and strobed between different field densities
at the fastest speed; the named rule now stays put as the history grows.

Catalog regressions check the mark roles, increasing lightness, mono visibility,
and remaining dichromacy distinctions. The current source-bound color audit
and flagship image goldens follow this intentional rendering change. Audio
golden metrics retain their existing contract.

## An active milestone, with honest boundaries

The package minor names active Sensory Alpha work. Understanding Alpha's
qualifying study remains open independently. Human playtests are optional
feedback and do not gate this release or Sensory Alpha's machine exit.

Stable Sensory Alpha qualification still needs the closed physical display
pacing set, perceptual regression coverage, general Cabinet and HUD scaling,
and the remaining photosensitivity and color-vision decisions. Physical timing
receipts measure machines, not playtest participants. The optional GPU post
stack retains its existing promotion gate.
