# Numinous 0.4.0 alpha 5

A third source-blind playtest attacked the alpha 4 supersede claim and it held
on all seven staged rooms. What the round found instead was one failure wearing
two sets of clothes: the product answering a question the player had not asked.
This prerelease closes both, plus a third case found while verifying the claim.

## A doorway does not sell a graded call

`describe_room` is reachable before any play, so a blurb is the one part of a
staged room a player can read without committing anything. Three of them printed
the very call the room grades.

- The Coffee Cup named the shared cardioid, which is the Times Tables
  revelation and the thing `place_wager` is graded against.
- Buffon's Needle named pi, which is the number `number_wager` asks you to call.
- Parrondo's Trap named ABB, which is the policy `policy_wager` asks you to
  call.

The playtest reported the first. Auditing every blurb in the catalog for the
same class found the other two, both worse for being self-inflicted. All three
doorways now name the question and the strangeness and keep the answer, and a
catalog-wide regression fails if a future doorway prints a graded call.

## A landed goal does not lecture

Landing an ordinary room's goal returned that room's whole explanation in the
same reply. Missing stayed quiet, so succeeding is what opened the paragraph.
That reverses the promise made at the door, that understanding is offered later
and only if you ask, and it pays a solved room by taking the solving away.

A met goal now opens `reveal_room` rather than speaking through it. Nothing is
lost: having played, the player can ask, and the answer is unchanged. The seven
staged rooms still answer their own `aha_summon`, because that argument is the
player asking.

## A room does not claim a commitment nobody made

Found while proving the alpha 4 claim rather than reported. Kepler Areas,
Parrondo's Trap, and Nontransitive Dice formatted the experiment-earn path
through their CALLED sentence, so a player who named nothing read
`CALLED EXPERIMENT`, or `CALLED EXPERIMENT AGAINST B`. Inventing a commitment is
the same betrayal as dropping a real one, and it is the exact inverse of the
defect alpha 4 repaired. All seven staged rooms now name the experiment they
ran, and Double Pendulum joins the shared wording.

## Smaller repairs from the same round

- Arecibo's action says what its hand does. Width follows where along x a poke
  lands, but the action read CLICK LEFT OR RIGHT, so a player tapping the edges
  saw only the extremes and concluded the locking widths were unreachable.
- `list_rooms` starters carry id, title, and wing instead of bare ids, so a
  first room can be chosen and named without reading the catalog the doorway
  exists to spare the player.
- `PLAY.md` leads its MCP setup with the packaged binary. A reader holding a
  released archive was told to run `cargo` first, which needs the toolchain the
  archive exists to avoid.

## Claims corrected rather than defended

- The alpha 4 notes said a second wager and a post-summon wager are refused.
  Those are properties of one visit, true on the App and in the core state
  machine. The MCP face rebuilds the visit from each call's arguments by design,
  so it cannot show them, and the notes now say so.
- The Understanding Alpha contract described Game of Life's fourth call as
  carrying "the same earned reveal material". It never did, because that room
  has no goal. The gap is now stated in `UNDERSTANDING_STUDY.md`, along with the
  two ways to close it, and the choice is left to the registration ruling the
  cohort is already waiting on.

## Recorded, deliberately unbuilt

The playtest asked for a verb the tools do not have: **stay**. Every look is a
new stateless call, so a player sitting with one dark unlit point must re-ask
for it, and the room cannot know they never left. The same round named the wings
where rooms function perfectly and earn nothing. Both are design input for the
Mind's Seat and the keep-or-cut scorecard, and are recorded in `PLAYTESTS.md`
rather than answered here.

## Release boundary

Stable `0.4.0` still requires the complete Understanding Alpha evidence gate.
This prerelease corrects the playable artifact. It does not claim a qualifying
cohort result, a learning result, or a consciousness measurement.

## Release evidence

- The complete Windows gate passes 3,580 all-target Rust cases, with three
  expensive diagnostics ignored by the ordinary suite. Measured coverage is
  95.28 percent by region and 95.27 percent by line under the documented
  hardware and event-loop exclusions.
- All 2,937 deterministic App screens pass the rendered QA matrix.
- Warning-denied Clippy, formatting, documentation, house style, installer
  self-tests, Agent Plugin validation, coverage, packaging, provenance, and
  three-operating-system CI remain required by the publication workflow.
