# Numinous 0.4.0 alpha 7

The doorway stops explaining the room.

A fifth source-blind playtest of the published `0.4.0-alpha.6` binaries went
wandering rather than following the starters, and read the answer at three
doors before any picture had drawn. The same round found a room that had been
frozen since it was written and a new verb whose longest advertised use did not
fit the picture it was offered in. All three are fixed and guarded.

## A doorway names the question

`describe_room` is safe to call first, and `PLAY.md` and `initialize` both
promise it never returns the explanation. The alpha.5 release made that true for
staged rooms whose call is graded. It was still false for ordinary rooms, and
the tester found three by curiosity alone: Kaprekar named 6174, The Fastest Fall
named the cycloid, and The First Rain named the percolation threshold. Each of
those is the discovery the room exists to let a player make.

Auditing every doorway in the catalog against its own reveal found ten more,
including the Basel problem printing its closed form and the Busy Beaver
printing BB(5). All thirteen now name the question and the strangeness without
the answer.

```
before  Every mixed 4-digit number falls to 6174 in at most seven steps.
after   Feed it any four-digit number you like and watch where the cascade
        stops.
```

Two guards hold the line. A mechanical one fails any doorway that states a
multi-digit value its own reveal states, carrying a short list of the numbers a
doorway may legitimately share with a stated reason for each, so a citation year
and an apparatus count are not mistaken for an answer. The existing enumerated
guard grows to cover named answers, because no arithmetic catches "cycloid".

The tone came from the round too. Hilbert's Hotel and Goldbach's Comet were the
two doorways the tester singled out as doing it right: ask at the door, let the
picture answer.

## A jam that moves

Phantom Jam was reported dead, and it was. The same ring at every phase, no cell
answering a touch, and a mean speed of exactly zero.

The cause was density. Forty-eight cars on a unit ring left a mean headway of
0.0208 while every driver in the model refused to move below 0.028, so every car
chose a target speed of zero on the first step and the ring was frozen well
before anything drew. A parking lot cannot have a phantom jam, and the phantom
jam is the whole room.

The ring is now an optimal-velocity model with a car length, tuned so the evenly
spread ring sits on the unstable side of the response curve and clots because of
the rule rather than because anything scripted it. What it shows is its own
claim:

```
t=0.0  CARS +0.75  JAM -0.44  PACKED@98%
t=0.4  CARS +0.90  JAM -0.44  PACKED@66%
t=1.0  CARS +0.91  JAM -0.48  PACKED@15%
```

Cars go forward. The jam goes backward. The room prints both signed numbers and
never says which way a jam travels.

Sweeping the catalog for the same shape found one more. Six rooms draw one
picture at every phase; five answer the hand and promise nothing about a dial.
The sixth, The Busy Beaver, told players "t extends the step budget" while the
budget opened past the step at which the machine halts, so the dial did nothing
and the room showed a block that had already stopped. That is the room the
tester called dull. The phase now walks the machine through its own run: the
tape fills as you turn the dial and the stop is a moment you reach. A catalog
guard asks the narrower question that can be dishonest, which is whether a
doorway naming a dial has one.

## A stay that fits

`dwell` advertises two to eight looks. The render budget admitted exactly four
at the canvas a room draws when nobody asks for a size, so the first stay the
tester attempted, the longest one the schema names, in the picture just handed
over, was an error. The refusal quoted the cap and nothing else, which a caller
who never chose a size cannot act on.

The budget is now derived from the advertisement rather than chosen beside it,
so the longest stay fits the default canvas. A refusal now names the canvas it
is judging, what that canvas costs per look, and how many looks do fit.

## A melody that can be heard

`sing_expression` answered a curve with a table of frequencies. The tester made
two hills, could see the shape, and reported that making had collapsed back into
reading.

Every note now carries the step taken to reach it:

```
note  2:   659.9 Hz ( E5)  [up 702 cents, perfect fifth, 3:2 off by -0.3]
note  3:   232.1 Hz (A#3)  [down 1809 cents]
note  5:   880.0 Hz ( A5)  [up 2400 cents, octave, 4:1 off by +0.0]
```

The size in cents is a measurement of the two frequencies. The name and the
ratio are search results, offered only when something sits close enough, and the
ratio always carries its error, so a reader can tell what was heard from what
was merely nearby. Simplicity bounds the ratio search rather than tolerance
alone, because a ratio is a claim about consonance: allow enough complexity and
every step finds one. A semitone is therefore offered no ratio, since none
explains it, and an equal-tempered major third is reported as the fourteen cents
sharp of 5:4 that it is.

A perfect fifth is 3:2 whether it arrives through a cochlea or a parser. The
documentation has said so for a while; the tool now does.

## Also in this release

- **The Only Move** enters the catalog, which is why the tester could not find
  it: the room landed after the tag they were holding. A machine keeps a three
  by three grid and answers every touch from its own best-move set, and which
  of the eight lines count as a win is the dial.
- The compact `list_rooms` sentence names its starters instead of listing bare
  ids, which had sent a text-only reader into the catalog the short mode exists
  to spare them.
- The Berry Phase doorway poses its loop instead of captioning it.
- Two App postcard tests wiped a fixed temp directory on entry, so two
  concurrent runs of the suite deleted each other's scratch files.

## What this round did not close

The tester's wander verdicts named three rooms dull, two mute, and one dead.
Only the dead one is fixed here. A room that draws correctly and earns nothing
is the keep-or-cut scorecard's subject, and 246 of the catalog's doorways are
still under seventy characters of textbook caption, which is the same finding at
catalog scale. That is open and stated as open.

## Release boundary

Stable `0.4.0` still requires the complete Understanding Alpha evidence gate.
This prerelease fixes reported defects and adds no cohort result, learning
result, or consciousness measurement.
