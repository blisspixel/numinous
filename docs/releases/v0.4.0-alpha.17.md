# Numinous 0.4.0 alpha 17

This release corrects several room models, keeps a reopened Studio experiment
intact while you edit it, and refreshes the public screenshots from the App.
Understanding Alpha remains active; the catalog is not independently certified.

## Mathematics that survives a closer look

- Kepler's sun, orbital clock, and swept areas now agree. Small nonzero
  eccentricities correctly mean faster motion near the sun.
- Lotka-Volterra follows positive population trajectories with measured
  conservation error, and Lorenz uses a shared fourth-order numerical step
  checked against analytic limits and refinement.
- Spherical Harmonics corrects two misnormalized modes and checks all exposed
  pairwise inner products and the addition theorem.
- Lissajous keeps unit-amplitude curves, correct aspect, and the same frequency
  pair in its picture, readout, and sound. Live frequency changes preserve the
  intended note throughout the transition.
- Standing Wave keeps one common wave speed across its harmonic modes.
  Repeated selections retain their clock, and each mode has an equal share of
  the drag range.
- Simple Pendulum closes its swing contours, shows both separatrix branches,
  contains rotations, and admits small swings. Its energy portrait and status
  agree on the selected regime.
- Braess uses the traffic equilibrium appropriate to the chosen demand.
  Wet Oracle describes the field it simulates without promising an optimal route.

The equations, regression evidence, approximation bounds, and remaining gaps
are recorded in [the mathematical review](https://github.com/blisspixel/numinous/blob/v0.4.0-alpha.17/docs/MATHEMATICS.md).

## Keep the experiment you chose

Opening a Studio capsule preserves its saved window and parameter through
edits, incomplete drafts, repair, pitch-map changes, and leave/return. An
untouched preview keeps its identity; an edit records its remix parent.

F4 shares the creation, native link, postcard, and a MIDI melody. The same
Standard MIDI File export is available through `numinous sing 'sin(t)' --out song.mid`
and the MCP `sing_expression` tool with `midi: true`. MIDI uses one voice,
12-TET keys, and a declared pitch-bend range for the residual cents. It preserves
the declared duration, including trailing silence, while quantizing event times.
WAV keeps the native synthesized voice and overlapping envelopes.

Version 4 capsules add editable prose credit. A fork offers credit from its
parent's recorded identity; the player can keep, replace, or clear that text.
The native lineage link remains separate. Older capsule formats still reopen.

Journal references also preserve what was selected. Erasing and recreating a
journal cannot silently substitute another encounter with a reused local ID.

## Something to investigate and give away

[Returning home](https://github.com/blisspixel/numinous/blob/v0.4.0-alpha.17/docs/experiments/returning-home.md) offers three portable Studio
creations: a full return, an ideal irrational near return, and a repeated
position with reversed velocity. Compare them, change a formula, and keep or
share a branch. The proposed in-app capability quest remains unbuilt.

[Route Lab](https://github.com/blisspixel/numinous/blob/v0.4.0-alpha.17/docs/ROUTE_LAB.md) develops the next visual routing idea: watch a
shortest-path search, improve a delivery tour, and compare it with a proven
small-map optimum. It is a design with an exact worked example, not a shipped
solver.

The long-term direction remains ambitious and explicit about uncertainty:
usable understanding, beauty, choice, and player-owned continuity. Research
on enjoyment and digital consciousness informs the design without certifying
what any participant experiences.
