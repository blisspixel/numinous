# Numinous 0.4.0 alpha 22

A second mathematical treatment, and the corrections an external playtest of
alpha 21 asked for.

## Times Tables, derived

The flagship room now has an authored Mathematics treatment, the second in the
catalog. It states the construction exactly, then derives the envelope of the
chord family in closed form:

```text
E(u) = (m*exp(i*u) + exp(i*m*u)) / (m+1)
```

The derivation checks that the derived point lies on its own chord rather than
only satisfying the tangency condition, identifies the curve as an epicycloid by
an explicit rotation, and proves the cusp count is one fewer than the multiplier
twice over: from the rolling-circle ratio, and from where the traced speed
vanishes. Two worked cases give the cardioid and the nephroid with numbers.

A separate block says what the screen actually draws, which is not the envelope.
Indices are rounded, so a non-integer multiplier draws a perturbed sample of the
ideal family. Only some chords are drawn. The closing argument holds for integer
multipliers. The envelope is the shape a player's eye assembles from a finite
picture, and the text says so rather than implying the curve is on the screen.

The derivation is the project's own. The two cited sources are cited for the
definition of the epicycloid and its cusp count, not for the envelope result.
Four tests check the mathematics rather than the prose, so wording that drifts
from the proof fails the build.

This treatment is English only. The Lissajous Japanese draft was independently
reviewed and this text was not, so a Japanese request resolves to English and
reports that the translation is unavailable.

## Counts read correctly, on every face

An external playtester found the wing doorway in the protocol face still reading
`(1 rooms)` for the two single-room wings, on a release whose notes claimed that
class was fixed. The sweep behind that claim had covered only the App. Every
prose site where a count can reach one now routes through one shared helper: the
wing doorway, the compact play summary that reported cells changed, and the App
console search. Terse instrument readouts such as `pairs=3` are deliberately
unchanged, because there the noun is a field name the status parsers read.

Two claims in the alpha 21 notes were wrong and are corrected. There are two
single-room wings, not three, because Tetractys is a hidden room. The Journey
play count is an App overlay, which is why a tester working over the protocol
could not find it, and the player manual now says so.
