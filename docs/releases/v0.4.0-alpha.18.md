# Numinous 0.4.0 alpha 18

This release makes four dynamical rooms more faithful experiments and gives
Studio one explicit parameter across its picture, sound, and saved creations.
Understanding Alpha remains active, with its learning and wider review gates open.

## Finite experiments with measured evidence

- Henon-Heiles starts at the energy you selected. Its readout distinguishes an
  open escape barrier from an orbit reaching the numerical boundary, without
  inferring chaos from energy or size.
- Van der Pol recognizes the exact resting equilibrium and compares moving
  paths on shared axes. Its reported amplitude covers the full observed range,
  including the initial transient.
- Duffing measures the same 120-unit trace it draws. The drive is evaluated at
  each numerical stage's time, and large motion no longer earns a chaos label.
  A large response that repeats at the forcing period is a tested counterexample.
- Gray-Scott preserves its initial patch when you repeat a tuning. Phase selects
  elapsed time at fixed rates; the readout measures the chemical field rather
  than guessing a pattern class or bifurcation.

These rooms now use the shared fourth-order integration method with explicit
horizons and numerical checks. Analytic limits, energy and reaction balances,
independent endpoint references, and refinement tests complement reproducibility.
The [mathematical review](https://github.com/blisspixel/numinous/blob/v0.4.0-alpha.18/docs/MATHEMATICS.md)
records equations, sampled domains, error budgets, and their limits. These are
bounded corrections, not independent certification of the 355-room catalog.

The portraits preserve coordinate proportions and stay clear of compact App
chrome. Gray-Scott averages concentrations before choosing their displayed mark
and paints each pixel once, removing false brightness when the lattice shrinks.

## Tune something you can keep

In Studio, Up/Down change `a` by 0.25 and Home restores 1. Controller Up/Down
and Reset perform the same actions using your bindings. Drawing, live melody,
postcard, capsule, and MIDI all use that value and the same window.

Opened values keep their exact precision. Deliberate parameter edits retain
the saved window and record the remix parent; unchanged resets and held keyboard
repeats do not restart the melody. Recipes restore the default window and `a` and
blend only across compatible numerical settings. Auto describes its approximate
presentation timing honestly, without claiming musical phrase alignment.

Exact parameter readouts and paused-preview guidance fit both supported App
sizes. The public screenshots are refreshed from the current App rendering.

## Update

```text
numinous update
```

installed CLI/MCP smoke checks, source-bound provenance, and SBOM artifacts.
