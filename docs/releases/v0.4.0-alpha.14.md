# Numinous 0.4.0 alpha 14

The goal on the board can be won.

Alpha 13 made every click in The Only Move count, but its posted goal to win
one game remained practically unreachable. The one winnable rulebook was a
single stop among 255 on a continuous dial. A source-blind player tried the
visible board repeatedly, finished games, and never found it.

This release gives that rulebook a broad detent around `t=0.40`. Three visible
cell choices can now beat the machine there. The rulebook stays on `t`, an
empty hand still earns nothing, and all 254 other nonempty rulebooks remain
reachable in their established order.

## Every rejected poke has a name

The Only Move used to report only how many pokes were wasted. That was not
enough to learn from a game against an opponent that replies immediately.

The status now identifies rejected pokes by their one-based input positions.
The exact nine-cell sweep from the playtest reports `WASTE #2,#4,#6` beside its
earned `WON 1`. Consecutive positions compact into ranges, and longer feedback
stays inside the room footer's fixed width.

Core tests preserve the exact winning call, prove every rulebook remains
reachable through the phase dial, and still exhaust all 256 rulebooks to show
that exactly one is first-player winnable. A real stdio test repeats the
published play through the packaged MCP face and requires `goalMet: true`.

## ACROSS spins, UP lifts

The same playtest found that a horizontal drag across The 720 Degree Room's
stone row reported a quarter turn and `OVER`. The gesture never rose. Its
height was being mistaken for upward travel, and its landing column was being
discarded when the spin was read.

A horizontal drag now uses its landing column and has no lift or win. Its
status is `TURNS 2.00  TWIST +2.00`. A multi-point gesture must visibly rise
before it carries the belt over. The compact stone poke and a real upward pass
still land `TURNS 2.00  OVER  FLAT`, and the 132-cell braid remains in the
finished frame.

## Boundaries this release keeps

The three-door threshold, caller-paced Show, exact temporal replay, named wager
boundary, real WAV channel, and path-free `portable-1` capsule are unchanged.
Audio remains a sound sent, with whether it is heard left to the client.

This was the tenth source-blind packaged round. It is formative product
evidence, not the qualifying Understanding Alpha cohort. Stable 0.4.0 still
requires that registered study and its independent audit. The round and its
open boundaries are recorded in `docs/PLAYTESTS.md`; release gates are in
`VERIFY.md`.
