# Numinous 0.4.0 alpha 20

Play a room, then choose how deeply to study it. Explain, Notes, and Mathematics
are available directly, without a level or visit requirement. The App, CLI,
and MCP read the same content.

## Curiosity chooses the depth

Press E or ? in room play, or choose Explain in the Cabinet. The reader keeps
the room's tuning, phase, and accepted input history. Its three depth tabs stay
visible while the text scrolls. Closing returns to the room or Cabinet that
opened it. A deliberate language change saves only that preference.

The seven staged App experiments are now a separate choice through U or
Cabinet Experiment. Enter advances a connection when that experiment offers
one. Reading remains available before any prediction and during the discovery.
Existing CLI and MCP reveal commands retain their progression contracts.

## The first mathematical treatment

Lissajous now has a full treatment of its ideal oscillators: full-state periods,
position-only returns, phase geometry, torus motion, occupation measure, and
approximate recurrence. Worked examples and primary references accompany the
derivations. The text distinguishes the continuous model from binary64 values,
a finite drawn trace, and the room's sound mapping.

English and a labeled Japanese translation draft share equations and reference
identities. Other rooms expose their existing explanations and notes; their
unwritten Mathematics treatments are explicitly unavailable. Hawaiian and
Klingon requests currently report English fallback. This is the beginning of
reviewed content coverage, not complete App localization.

The native reader uses bundled fonts for case-preserving prose, Japanese, and
linear mathematical notation. It wraps text and retains each depth's position.
The workspace compiler minimum is now Rust 1.89. Original font licenses and
notices accompany every native package.

## Shared truth across faces

```text
numinous study lissajous --depth mathematics
numinous study lissajous --locale ja --block lissajous.recurrence --json
```

MCP exposes `study_room` with the same locale, depth, and block selection.
Structured responses report translation availability per block. Both study
faces are checked as real processes against disposable player profiles.

Six rooms now grade typed numerical channels without depending on English
label order. Existing decimal rounding, seeds, tolerances, and scores remain
stable. A prediction also verifies that its exact offered phase can be graded.

See [Study](https://github.com/blisspixel/numinous/blob/v0.4.0-alpha.20/docs/STUDY.md)
for controls and the content contract. Understanding Alpha remains active;
catalog-wide mathematical review, broader translations, learning outcomes,
and sensory promotion still have evidence to earn.

## Update

```text
numinous update
```

The release pipeline builds and audits four native packages with checksums,
installed CLI/MCP smoke checks, source-bound provenance, and SBOM artifacts.
The advisory gates carry one documented temporary exception for the
unmaintained `ttf-parser` crate behind the text renderer, used here only on
the embedded fonts; its exit condition is recorded in the engineering guide.
