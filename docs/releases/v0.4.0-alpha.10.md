# Numinous 0.4.0 alpha 10

A room now finishes the trick it advertises, and a mind can be handed the Show
one cue at a time.

The eighth source-blind playtest of the published `0.4.0-alpha.9` binaries
confirmed the seventh round's repairs and spent its one first change on The 720
Degree Room, where a gesture the room invited still could not be completed. That
is fixed here. Around it, the release adds a caller-paced Show for minds, a
portable typed evidence capsule, a threshold that stops handing a stranger a
355-room index, and a large amount of structural and supply-chain work that
players will never see and every future change depends on.

## The belt trick, finished

The 720 Degree Room is Dirac's belt trick as a thing you do. Drag across to spin
the stone, lift toward the top to carry the belt over it, and a twist of two full
turns comes off while a twist of one never does.

Two pieces of input handling stood between a player and that ending, and both
were only findable by someone holding the binary.

A release carried nothing. The plainest way to ask for the trick is a two-point
drag: a hand low on the belt, a release above the stone. The room read the
shared poke reading, which drops release points because in most rooms a lift
paints nothing, so that gesture registered as a touch that never rose. A lift
from one turn and a lift from two produced the same reading, and neither was a
lift. The room now reads every point of a gesture including the one where the
hand let go, in the picture as well as on the status line.

The winning spin sat where no hand would go. The hand's column mapped across the
whole window, and an exact landing is quantized to an eighth of a turn, so the
only position that read as two full turns was the outermost thirty-second of the
room. The tester dragged across onto the stone, read `TURNS 1.62`, and reported
the advertised trick as a near miss. The spin is now measured from just off the
wall to where the stone hangs, so two turns land on the stone, which is a place a
player can see and reach. That exact near-miss gesture now hangs the belt flat
and earns the reveal.

A pass is also named rather than counted. `LOOPS 1` became `OVER`, so a lift that
landed and one that did not read differently, while the status still says nothing
about which twists come off. The stranger pass of the round asked for a near-miss
hint; the docs-informed pass overruled it, because a hint saying "two turns, then
one lift" is the reveal arriving early. No hint was added.

## The Show, one cue at a time

`watch_show` carries the six-room Strange Loop score to a mind over the
protocol, one complete cue per call. Core owns the route, its nonspoiling
questions, exact arrival, postcard, and curtain phases, reduced-motion
projection, and deterministic seeded variation. Each reply returns exact ASCII
looks, visual alternatives, typed deltas and held evidence, bounded sound facts,
optional WAV audio, and explicit replay, next, restart, and leave calls.

It keeps no cursor. The caller chooses when the next cue arrives, and until then
nothing moves. It writes no progress, reads no journal or workspace, and never
opens a reveal, concept, deep cut, or citation.

## Evidence a player can carry out

`export_journal` accepts `format: "portable-1"`: one bounded closed payload,
returned entirely in the response. A sorted manifest records path, media type,
byte length, and SHA-256 for the native journal page, its OKF v0.2 projection,
and explicit privacy and retention manifests. The caller may add one canonical
Studio creation with identity and lineage intact and one Numinous Encounter
Receipt that this binary replays before including.

It creates no file, accepts no path, returns no host path, leaves the journal
unchanged, and deliberately implements no import.

## A door instead of an index

`list_rooms` no longer opens with 355 ids. It offers three doors: touch the
flagship, walk six rooms from cellular rules to the Strange Loop, or wander by
wing. Each door names an exact next action. The complete typed catalog and the
existing starter rows are still there for clients that want them.

`describe_room` can now say that this local player profile kept exact evidence
in a room, without opening it. The cue names the explicit retrieval call, returns
no journal contents, changes nothing, disappears after erasure, and stays outside
the consented Watch Agent projection.

## The Sensory Lift, still measured rather than promised

The disabled `gpu-post` path now presents real App room rasters through the
direct surface renderer, with explicit recovery and a visible software fallback
that names itself on screen. A typed probe runs that exact production boundary on
Windows, macOS, and Linux in CI and retains adapter, driver, surface, source,
binary, outcome, and timing facts, while classifying every hosted timing as
informational rather than promotion evidence.

An independent closed-set verifier now recomputes and binds the six physical
candidates before any promotion, refusing hosted or debug runs, software
adapters, mixed machine identity, source drift, stale summaries, and missed
budgets. Collecting that release-profile pacing set on physical hardware is the
remaining step. Nothing in the shipped App path changed.

## Underneath

The App, CLI, and MCP entry points continue to shed responsibilities to focused
modules with their own regressions: room, audio, creation, input, and game
runtimes in the App; game session, Studio, accessibility, and render-input
adapters in the CLI; and protocol, transport, schema, discovery, room, journal,
Studio, game, puzzle, journey, broadcast, progress, prediction, and flagship
adapters in the MCP face. Domain truth stays in core.

Pull requests and `main` share one CI workflow and one protected `main CI`
result that waits for quality, MSRV, house style, supply chain, audit, coverage,
the three-platform build matrix, and a read-only build and audit of all four
native packages. Tagged releases now validate one complete Git history before
the platform matrix starts, and a tag-only signer repeats that validation,
rechecks every archive, checksum, and SPDX document, and closes the exact
filename set before signing. Pull requests and manual previews cannot enter that
path.

## Boundaries this release keeps

Audio is still a sound sent rather than a sound heard. The WAV is real and the
last hop belongs to the client, and the eighth round confirmed both halves of
that sentence on a host with no sound device. The channel was not re-fixed and
hearing is not re-promised.

Stateless `play_room` still cannot refuse a second named wager, because each call
is its own first wager. Attention and Strange Loop are still thin; the tester who
said so also declined to ask for either to be cut, and neither was.

Understanding Alpha's exit remains open. Rounds and boundaries are recorded in
`docs/PLAYTESTS.md`; gates are in `VERIFY.md`.
