# Numinous 0.4.0 alpha 27

Studio reads a formula the player already wrote, and a question can outlive the visit.

## A slope beside the graph

An open graph `sin(a*x)` grows `a*cos(a*x)`. The opening formula
`sin(a*x) + x/3` grows `a*cos(a*x)+1/3`. An open graph `cos(a*x)` grows
`-a*sin(a*x)`. The App draws and sings both curves on one axis. CLI
`open-studio` and MCP report the same source. The capsule, the postcard,
and `melody.mid` stay the player's formula. `sin(x)`, `cos(x)`, a product
of two curves, and a named slider have no slope reading.

## The first term of a sum

A path or a graph of up to twelve oscillators grows its first term and
sounds one tone per recognized frequency. Frequency 1 is 110 Hz. A
thirteenth term has no partial reading. The capsule stays the source.

## One question you can keep

A project keeps one question, a closed next call, and an optional Studio
creation. MCP can keep, import, correct, and preview that chain. CLI
`numinous project resume` prints the preview, and `--apply` writes the
question into that process only. The App Cabinet shows The Question when
the chain is present. Enter starts a present creation. The project file,
the journal, and the journey stay unchanged.

Understanding Alpha remains active, and its exit still waits on a
registered study rather than on any code.
