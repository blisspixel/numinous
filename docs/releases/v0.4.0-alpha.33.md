# Numinous 0.4.0 alpha 33

A long room voice keeps its notes instead of closing the agent door.

## A useful answer when the WAV cannot fit

Mandelbrot's full mathematical voice lasts 48 seconds. Asking for it over MCP
used to fail the whole reply at the encoded audio limit, losing the notes and
the Overture cue, then suggesting a shorter sound the tool could not request.

`listen_room`, `watch_show`, and `sing_expression` now keep the successful
result when a WAV is too large. Notes, Show looks, receipts, and continuation
remain available. `audioOmission` states the reason, duration, rate, exact
requested bytes, and unchanged limit. Show carries it under `segment.sound`
and declares the field in schema version 3. Full and compact replies both
explain the omission. No sound was delivered or claimed heard.

The size check uses core's exact WAV length before synthesizing PCM. Short
WAV attachments and MIDI retain their existing behavior. Full-length files
remain available through the CLI:

```text
numinous sonify mandelbrot --layer mathematical --t 0 --out mandelbrot.wav
```

PLAY.md explains how to carry another room's phase, variation, and hand, or a
Studio expression's window and scale, into its local pre-master export.
