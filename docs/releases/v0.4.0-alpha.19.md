# Numinous 0.4.0 alpha 19

Studio now preserves the shape of a parametric creation across the App,
terminal, postcards, and Gallery. This release also refreshes the public
presentation with a simple cyan mark, current screenshots, and clearer controls.

## A circle stays a circle

Earlier Studio views fitted x and y independently. That could make a circle
and a 4:1 ellipse look alike. A shared planar projection now uses equal
physical coordinate units, so the distinction survives into saved pictures
and thumbnails. Constant coordinates remain centered, isolated finite points
remain visible, and undefined samples break the path.

The checks measure actual pixels and terminal characters, including complete
App panels, postcards, and Gallery tiles. Extreme ranges that cannot be
represented faithfully are refused. A fitted, sampled view still has limits:
uniformly enlarging a circle can leave its fitted picture unchanged, and a
finite trace is not a proof about the continuous curve.

The optional [Shape and scale experiment](https://github.com/blisspixel/numinous/blob/v0.4.0-alpha.19/docs/experiments/shape-and-scale.md)
starts with two portable creations. Stretch a circle, compare uniform scaling,
then name and share a version. A deeper area question is there when wanted.
The [mathematical review](https://github.com/blisspixel/numinous/blob/v0.4.0-alpha.19/docs/MATHEMATICS.md)
records the projection contract and its evidence.

## A clearer front door

- The Cabinet leads the README screenshots, followed by rooms and a full Studio
  view. All eight plates come from the current composed App rendering.
- Studio keeps F4 Share visible beside the creation. Its showcased formula
  responds to the parameter control.
- Kepler's ordinary text has brighter amber, with measured contrast rising
  from 2.70:1 to 4.97:1 on the shared stage.
- A cyan circle with one open band replaces the old identity mark. A single
  SVG source supplies the PNG and native Windows and macOS icons.

## Depth and language, the next work

The updated pedagogy and localization plans make the direction explicit:
play on entry, choose an explanation, and request rigorous mathematics without
a level requirement. A shared reader, language-independent mathematical facts,
Unicode layout and input, and reviewed translations remain implementation work.
Japanese, Hawaiian, and Klingon are not yet supported product languages.

Understanding Alpha remains active. These corrections do not close its learning,
catalog-wide mathematical review, accessibility, or sensory gates.
