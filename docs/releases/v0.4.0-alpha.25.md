# Numinous 0.4.0 alpha 25

A language of curves becomes a language of fields, and a packaged player
can follow the Studio doors that were already built.

## Fields over the plane

Formula Jam could draw a graph or a path. It can now draw a field. Type
`z^2 - 1` or `x^2 + y^2 - 1` and the same instrument shows a character
plate instead of a curve. Three readings of one expression: phase, where
the value points; height, how big it is; and zero, where it is nothing,
with a third answer when a cell cannot be decided.

Capsules write `NUMINOUS_STUDIO 5` with the requested window and reading.
CLI, MCP, and the App plot, save, reopen, and fork them. Fields are seen
first: they have no melody yet. Four bundled capsules, family
`three-readings`, compare a simple zero, a pole, a proved circle, and
the same circle read as height. `PLAY.md` and the skill name the ids.

## Packaged-player honesty already on main

Returning home and Shape and scale open by id from a packaged install.
A plotted experiment names a way to keep it. A kept creation names a way
to remix. Lissajous study names the bundled Returning home ids a player
can actually open. The Understanding collector expects the keep door.

Understanding Alpha remains active, and its exit still waits on a
registered study rather than on any code.
