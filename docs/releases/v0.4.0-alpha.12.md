# Numinous 0.4.0 alpha 12

The cabinet finally tells you how big it is.

This release answers the ninth source-blind round in full, and then closes a gap
that had been open since the catalog passed a couple of dozen rooms: a player at
the App had no way to find out that the other three hundred existed.

## A door onto the catalog

The App stepped one room per press through 355 with wraparound. The only jump
was a console opened with a key nothing in the App mentioned, into which a
player typed an id nothing in the App would teach them. The menu offered modes,
games, settings and controls, and nothing anywhere said the catalog was larger
than what an arrow key reaches.

Modes now opens Wings. It lists every wing the catalog has, and one way back out
to the whole cabinet. Choosing a wing lands on its first room, keeps the arrows
inside it, and says how many rooms it holds, which is the first time the App
states that number out loud. Leaving is the last entry in the same list, so a
player who wandered in can get out without knowing a key. The controls screen
also names the console now, spelled rather than drawn because the bitmap font
has no glyph for a backtick and a blank column teaches nothing.

Wing navigation walks a set of catalog indices rather than a range, because the
catalog is ordered for arrival and a wing's rooms are scattered through it, so
stepping by one would leave a wing immediately. Three properties are tested: the
wings partition the catalog exactly, every wing walks end to end and closes its
loop, and a room reached by some other route steps to the nearest room of the
wing rather than dumping the player at its start.

The wing reading lives in core, shared with the protocol face, which had been
building the same list for its own wander door. A list built twice is two lists
that can disagree about what the catalog contains.

## The ninth round, answered

The belt trick could be finished and finishing it looked exactly like never
having played, because two turns come off and leave a flat belt and a flat belt
is what the room starts as. The room now keeps the twist that came off, drawn
behind the belt that hangs now: from two turns a double braid behind two flat
lines, and from one turn the two lying exactly on top of each other, because a
pass reverses a single twist into one that looks the same. That contrast is the
double cover, drawn rather than told.

The Only Move reports the game it is in, `YOURS 1  MINE 1  OPEN 7`, instead of a
row of zeros after every click. A room whose verb is take a cell owes a player
the cells.

Two claims that were no longer true are gone: the README's promise of sound
returned as sound, which had survived two rounds after the correction landed
everywhere else, and the 720 doorway naming Dirac's belt trick and telling a
player what to count.

## Ten connections, placed

Rooms that have earned their explanation now hand over the room they rhyme with.
The full verified set is in: the qubit and the Hopf fibration, the hanging chain
and the soap film, the cycloid that is its own evolute, the three enumerations of
the rationals that share one mediant, the parabola and the corner and the digit
shift that are one system, the angle and the curve a sunflower needs both of, the
bell and the gasket that are one triangle read by size and by parity, the first
collision against the last empty bin, Gauss's diary entry of 1799, and the two
faces of one function at an easy point and along a hard line.

## The world holds still, provably

A room's state is a function of its phase and its touches, phases carry no
duration, and input history is bounded by count and never by age. Nothing decays
and nothing times out, so a visit resumed after a second and a visit resumed
after a decade meet the same room. That had been true since the beginning and
nothing enforced it. A scan now does, with file locking as the one stated
exception because stale-lock recovery genuinely needs the host clock and touches
no room, no phase and no grade.

## What the doorway says

The math-only doorway used to end "it is the same for every mind," which is a
sentence it cannot cash, because all three tiers of visitor assume something
that reads symbols, holds a number concept, issues a call, persists across at
least two calls, and builds a model from what comes back. It now ends "the same
for every mind that can take a turn," which is true, narrower, and still a wider
door than most software has.

## Boundaries this release keeps

Audio is still a sound sent rather than a sound heard. Stateless `play_room`
still accepts a second named wager as a new first wager. Three doors are still
not a single invitation. The App's wing browser is the wander door only; the
other two doors of the protocol threshold remain protocol-side. Understanding
Alpha's exit remains open. Rounds and boundaries are in `docs/PLAYTESTS.md`;
gates are in `VERIFY.md`.
