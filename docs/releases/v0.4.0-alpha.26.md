# Numinous 0.4.0 alpha 26

The Studio learns rhythm and melody as formulas, and the front door says
the true numbers.

## Pattern Studio, first views

`euclid(3,8)` places three onsets as evenly as possible on eight steps.
The graph is 1 on a hit and 0 on a rest, and that one graph reads three
ways: tracker marks `x..x..x.`, a numbered step grid the App draws as
cells, and a piano roll of the sung MIDI voice, pitch over time. Type the
marks directly, `x..x..x.` or `pat(x..x..x.)`, and they are the formula.
`note("c e g")` writes named MIDI pitches the same way, with `.` a rest,
and the voice sings those pitches. Overlay layers a melody over a rhythm
with `&`. CLI, MCP, and the App plot, save, reopen, and fork all of it.

Bundled families: `euclidean` (`tresillo`, `three-against-five`) and
`notes` (`major-triad`, `octave-climb`).

## Knobs, overlays, and singing fields

A formula may bind extra identifiers besides `a`; each is a slider with a
value and a declared range, kept in the capsule. Two to four graphs share
one window with `sin(x) & cos(x)`; every graph sings in WAV and MIDI stays
the first curve. Height and phase fields now sing their reading along the
real axis; the zero reading stays silent because it is a proof.

Bundled families: `named-sliders` (`extra-knob`, `live-ratio`), `overlay`
(`the-parts`, `the-sum`), and `two-voices` (`closing-voices`,
`wandering-voices`), which draws the oscillators of Returning home as
graphs.

## True numbers at the front door

Two accidental wings are merged into the wings they sat beside, so
thirteen wings hold 355 rooms and the Mandelbrot set is filed under
Fractals. `VERIFY.md` says 41 MCP tools because that is what the tests
pin. The release gate now runs the two Sensory Lift contracts and the
MSRV check that `main CI` requires. The roadmap says what is built.

Dependency updates are opened by hand from this release on. No update bot
authors commits here.

Understanding Alpha remains active, and its exit still waits on a
registered study rather than on any code.
