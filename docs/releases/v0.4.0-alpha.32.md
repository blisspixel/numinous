# Numinous 0.4.0 alpha 32

A smoother instrument, a richer fractal, and the first correction from the
alpha 31 playtest.

## Light that shows the mathematics

Mandelbrot now has a continuous multicolor escape-time field, shared by the
CPU renderer and the native GPU path. Violet, indigo, teal, amber, and rose
ease into a uniform near-black outer field. The set's interior stays dark.
The former bright blue cutoff bands are gone. Closed-form tests for the main
cardioid and period-two bulb avoid redundant iteration inside the set.

Pickover keeps the map's mathematical bounds as its viewport. Small orbits
stay small instead of being magnified to fill the screen. Its unchanged
flash sweep passes through the App's measured speed ladder to 8x. Cellular
Automata, Julia, and Lambda Map remain disclosed as exceeding that proxy at
8x; the measurement does not certify flashing area or music-driven motion.

GPU startup tries primary graphics backends before initializing OpenGL. This
fixes a locally reproduced AMD driver teardown crash; CPU fallback remains.

## Motion and sound together

Room changes fade through the dark stage. Drag dials and Studio knobs glide
on critically damped springs while grading and saved values remain exact.
Reduced motion makes input immediate and uses a shorter plain fade. Rapid
changes wait briefly at the stage to keep arrivals separated.

Room scores and game cues share a modest reverb and a soft master limiter.
Notes bloom, pluck, or swell through shared envelopes, and loop releases wrap
without cutting their tails. Room changes wash the outgoing score into its
tail. Radio keeps its track and position, with a dry mix.

Settings now has separate Radio, Room Sound, and Effects levels under Master.
They persist alongside existing choices. Older preferences load with the new
levels at their default, and compact Settings keeps its controls visible.

## Honest Studio feedback

A bare unknown name such as `zzzzz` is refused on plot, sing, and keep, with a
hint for introducing a parameter inside a formula. It no longer becomes a
flat plot, an A3 drone, or a save door. Named parameters such as `sin(b*x)`
remain supported, as do constants, rhythms, and field variables.

Core Show direction, the Front Hall, and the caller-paced Overture are also
included. Native Overture playback and Vibe remain on the roadmap. The broader
Sensory Lift's physical platform qualification remains open, and Understanding
Alpha has not reached its stable exit.

## Update and play

```text
numinous update
```

For this feedback round, try Mandelbrot at rest and while diving, move between
rooms quickly and with reduced motion, listen to room changes and repeated
notes, and adjust each sound level in Settings. On MCP, retest `zzzzz` and a
real parameter formula. PLAY.md carries the packaged controls and known limits.
