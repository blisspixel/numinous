# Numinous 0.4.0 alpha 8

The melody arrives as a melody.

A sixth source-blind playtest of the published `0.4.0-alpha.7` binaries
confirmed the previous release's two headline fixes under direct reproduction,
then wrote the sentence six rounds have now ended on: "I still cannot hear the
two hills." It also named its own first ask, found a scoreboard still reciting
the answer its doorway had stopped giving, and found a hand that lifted and was
forgotten. All of it is closed and guarded.

## A mind down a pipe can hear

Every round has answered "I cannot hear it" with a better reading. Alpha.7 gave
each note the exact step taken to reach it, its size in cents, its
equal-tempered name, and the whole number ratio with the error on that ratio.
The verdict was precise and it was fair:

```
Making is no longer a frequency list I cannot interpret. It is still reading.
```

No further column was going to close that. A mind that reaches this house over
a protocol has no audio device to open, so the only way it can hear anything is
if the sound arrives as something it already knows how to receive, and the
protocol has a place for exactly that.

`sing_expression` and `listen_room` now take `audio: true` and return the sound
itself: a mono sixteen-bit WAV in an audio content block, beside the notation
and never instead of it, so a client that cannot pass audio to its model loses
nothing it had before.

```
sing_expression { "expr": "sin(x)+0.4*sin(3*x)", "notes": 32, "audio": true }
  -> content[0]  the reading, as before
  -> content[1]  { "type": "audio", "mimeType": "audio/wav", "data": "UklGR..." }
```

Core renders the file, header and all, with no dependency and no audio device,
because the point of this path is that it needs neither. The base64 encoder is
checked against the standard vectors and the header against its own size fields.
The reply describes what it sent, so a caller knows what it is holding without
decoding a megabyte to find out, and a sound too long for one message is refused
with the budget, its own seconds, and what to ask for instead.

Whether a sent sound is a heard one is the next round's question. It is no
longer ours to answer for the player.

## The rulebook is the dial

The tester found The Only Move by title, played it, swept `t` across sixteen
phases, and watched the board stay at eight lines the whole way. The rulebook
lived on `variation`, which a stranger has no reason to try. Their closing note
was unambiguous: make it the same dial every other room turns.

It is. The walk is ordered by the room's own argument rather than by the bitmask
counting up. Rulebooks descend by how many lines they count, so lines come off
as the dial turns, and inside one size they descend by how much of the square's
symmetry they keep. The one board a first player can win is the board that
treats every direction alike, so it is met near the front of its band rather
than stumbled on at stop 214.

And a rulebook is now a shape. Each counted line is stroked through the three
cells it joins, so eight lines is a star, the six rows and columns are a
lattice, and the winnable one is a square with an X through it. The two most
symmetric six-line boards sit next to each other on the dial looking equally
fair. Only one of them can be won.

The status names the mask, which is the number `variation` accepts, and the stop
out of 255, so the dial and the pin are the same room.

## A status may read the picture, not recite the reveal

Alpha.7 stopped ordinary doorways printing the fact their own reveal repeats.
The scoreboards were still doing it one room deeper, and the tester logged it as
an aside: "Play status can still print a number the doorway withheld."

Most of that list is honest. Kaprekar's cascade really does land where the
status says it landed. The Busy Beaver was not: it printed BB(5) on every frame,
including the first, before the tape had written a mark, and the tape never
draws that number at any phase. Sweeping the catalog found a worse one. Golden
Angle announced the golden angle at every phase while its own dial detuned the
picture out from under it, so the line was both the reveal arriving early and a
false reading of the head on screen.

```
before  GOLDEN ANGLE 137.5 DEG   CLICK: PLANT A SEED     (at every phase)
after   STEP 137.5 DEG   CLICK: PLANT A SEED             (at rest)
        STEP 143.2 DEG   CLICK: PLANT A SEED             (detuned)
```

The line is now mechanical: a value that survives every phase of the dial is a
recital, because nothing the player did or could do changed it, and a value that
moves with the dial is a reading. Four constants that are honest readings of a
picture that does not change carry a written reason each.

## The 720 Degree Room is a trick you can do

Two rounds called this room mute: "Status goes TWIST to FREE. The picture barely
turns." A room about turning that does not visibly turn is a defect, and the
old picture also claimed the belt was FREE at 720 degrees when in truth a twist
of two turns is removable rather than absent.

The belt is now drawn as its two edges. Where it has turned a quarter way the
edges meet; where it has turned half they have swapped sides; a flat belt is two
parallel lines. Counting crossings is counting half turns, so the twist is in
the picture.

And the trick is playable. Dragging across spins the stone; lifting toward the
top of the room carries the belt over it, which removes two full turns and turns
the stone not at all. From one turn you can lift all day and never reach flat.
From two, one lift clears it. That difference is the double cover, and it is now
something a player does rather than something the room tells them.

## One thing that is a boundary, not a bug

The tester held Phantom Jam's brake, let go, and read back the ring they had
never touched. The first instinct was that a jam should outlive the hand that
started it, since that is the room's whole subject.

The build disagrees for a reason older than the observation. A verb beginning
with HOLD makes a room a live hold across all three faces, and every such room is
held to ending its effect on release and on cancel. Letting go of a throttle is
meant to let go, and a stateless face that rebuilds a visit from its inputs has
no history for a released hand to persist into. Nothing is lost either: the jam
that outlives its cause is already what the dial shows, walking the clot from 98
percent of the ring round to 15 while every car drives the other way.

The contract stands. The boundary is written down rather than quietly bent.

## What stayed open

Two rooms are still called dull (Attention, Berry Phase) and one mute (Strange
Loop). 246 of the catalog's doorways remain under seventy characters of textbook
caption, which belongs to the keep-or-cut scorecard rather than to a release.
The Analysis plotter wing and the attractor drawer have not been walked since
the third round. The stateless MCP face still cannot refuse a second or
post-summon wager, which remains a property of a visit rather than a defect.
