# Numinous 0.5.0 alpha 4

The packaged play guide now opens with a multilingual welcome and direct App
and MCP starting instructions. It is shorter, links detailed controls and
workflows to their guides, and keeps accessibility disclosures and bundled
experiment references available offline. Play, study, games, and creation are
all welcome starting points.

The repository also gains a mathematical research atlas: readable paper notes,
pinned source evidence, selected construction insights, open questions, and
connections to rooms and proposed work. A Rust builder keeps the notes,
graph, coverage register, and offline viewer consistent. The atlas and full
research documents live in the repository; they are not added to the native
release archives.

The design direction is more concrete: real mathematical objects support
beautiful play and useful investigation. Proposed games explore recurrence,
correlation, routing counterexamples, transport, and spatial/spectral patterns.
A digital-play research review records current support and a proposed study of
freely chosen activity. These are design and research additions, not newly
shipped rooms, solver capabilities, or evidence of enjoyment.

Sensory Alpha remains active. Its existing qualification work and the separate
Understanding Alpha evidence requirements remain open. This release updates
the player documentation and research tooling without claiming those gates
have closed.

For an installer-created installation, run `numinous update`. For a loose
archive, download a fresh platform archive. Player-owned state is retained by
the managed update path.
