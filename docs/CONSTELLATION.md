# The Constellation: the meta-map as discovery, not checklist

**Designed direction, reviewed 2026-10-08.** A personal discovery map can record
encounters, questions and constructions while a complete mathematical map
remains freely browsable. Rooms, connections, study, inspectors, exact input
and exports are available immediately. Personal discovery changes highlighting,
never access. Enjoyment and transferable understanding are hypotheses to test.
`NORTH_STAR.md`, `CONSTRUCTIONS.md` and `PROGRESSION.md` own the surrounding
direction. The built offline paper atlas is a separate research tool described
in [RESEARCH_GRAPH.md](RESEARCH_GRAPH.md).

## The diagnosis

The initial Constellation idea was "collect a Constant on Revelation." That is a
checklist, which is exactly the XP treadmill the thesis forbids, wearing a
prettier hat. A checklist tells you chores remaining. A discovery map shows you
mysteries open. The whole redesign is that swap.

## The node model

Optional personal annotations, inspired by discovery maps:

- **Dark** (no encounter recorded): the room and its connections are available;
  the annotation makes no claim about the player's prior knowledge.
- **Touched** (interaction recorded): the node glows dim. This records an
  encounter without inferring what the player felt or understood.
- **Lit** (a construction, prediction or explanation recorded): the node and
  relevant edges gain personal highlighting. Their unhighlighted versions
  remain visible in the complete map.

The transition to Lit records an act from `PEDAGOGY.md` under its stated
conditions. It does not establish general understanding or mastery. Opening
an explanation remains a complete and freely available choice.

## Edges are insights you confirm

An edge names a specific relationship: identity under a stated map, a useful
prerequisite, shared construction, analogy, or proposed transfer. These have
different meanings. Similar pictures do not establish equality, and a shared
word such as self-similarity does not make several rooms instances of one
theorem. Record the source, conditions and limits behind a connection.

A player can optionally confirm a finite case by constructing it or comparing
the relevant quantities. That act may highlight the edge in their personal
map. A recognition click is evidence of that interaction, not a proof or a
measurement of transferable understanding. A useful door should also explain
what object can travel through it and what the next room lets the player do.

## Constants are regions, not trophies

Each fundamental constant (pi, e, phi, i, aleph-null, gamma) owns a colored
region of the sky. Meeting a constant tints its region; recorded encounters
and constructions fill it in. The Visual Era ceremony follows this progression
(historical order), so the sky and the whole app age up together, the era grain
and the meta-map advancing as one.

## Per-player layout

Because personal highlights follow discovery order, players' skies can differ.
Your map is a portrait of how *you* came to understand mathematics, not a
standardized completion grid. This is the single property that makes the meta
feel like a universe you explored rather than a form you filled, and it is why
the redesign is worth the build cost.

## The daily route plays on this board

The Daily Traverse (the run structure) is literally a path drawn across your
current sky, preferring edges that lead from a lit node to an adjacent dark one,
so it suggests an unexplored encounter. One featured seed per
day threads a handful of rooms along reviewed edges; your elegance at each stop
(from `CONSTRUCTIONS.md`) can suggest a lens for the next room, such as a
spectrum or a symmetry view. Those tools are also directly available at any
time. A suggested route has an ending, and another seed offers another route.

A daily seed is an optional invitation. Replaying it, choosing another route
and wandering remain unrestricted. There is no streak penalty, required wait
or earned access to a mathematical tool.

## The bottom is real

The Layer-4 lore payoff sits at the deepest cluster: a region that only lights
when enough of the sky connects, the Strange-Loop insight-chain terminating in
the discoverable endpoint. It is the one true gate in the whole map (everything
else reveals rather than blocks), and it must be designed now so the deepest
diggers arrive somewhere worthy. This is optional fictional lore, with no
mathematical knowledge, tool or room hidden behind it.

## Resolving the open questions in PROGRESSION.md

1. **How explicit should progression be?** A freely browsable complete map
   with optional personal discovery highlighting and local views to limit clutter.
2. **How hard to gate?** Reveal, do not block, to protect the Wanderer; only the
   Layer-4 bottom is truly gated.
3. **Shared vs bespoke elegance metric?** Bespoke per room, unified only by
   presentation (see `CONSTRUCTIONS.md`).

## Rendering bar

A janky web undercuts the thesis worse than no web. Ship it incrementally (the
data model and edge events first, then the animated sky), gated by the same
beauty QA every room faces, and rendered through the glow pipeline in
`SYNESTHESIA.md` so the sky is lit, not flat.
