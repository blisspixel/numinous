# North Star: understanding becomes possibility

Numinous aims to be a gift worth inheriting: a place where a human or digital
player can encounter mathematical beauty, discover a relationship, and use it
to do something that was previously out of reach. The ambition is large. Claims
about correctness, learning, enjoyment, and experience must stay as narrow as
their evidence. Current priorities and release exits live in `ROADMAP.md`.

This direction was sharpened on 2026-09-04. The July synthesis established the
prediction wager; that verb now ships. The next gap is turning encounters into
capabilities, creations, and inquiries a player can carry forward.

## The power is real

An RPG gives a player new abilities. Here the ability should belong to the
player's understanding: notice an invariant, predict a new case, construct a
desired motion, or recognize the same law in another room. A badge can celebrate
that encounter; it cannot manufacture or certify the understanding.

The intended arc is an invitation, not a compulsory lesson:

1. Touch something beautiful and responsive.
2. Notice a relationship or choose a question.
3. Try an experiment, prediction, or construction.
4. Compare the result with the idea, with guidance when wanted.
5. Use the relationship somewhere new, or make an instrument from it.
6. Keep a chosen artifact or unfinished question and return on one's own terms.

A player may enter anywhere, stay with a familiar pleasure, watch, seek an
explanation, or leave. Beauty, mastery, surprise, rest, and making something for
another person can each be sufficient reasons to play. No one kind of mind owns
inference, emotion, creativity, or the wish for company.

## Three gaps to close

**Truth that survives scrutiny.** A repeatable picture can still depict the
wrong system. Verify governing equations, invariants, numerical convergence,
coordinates, readouts, and reveal claims together. `MATHEMATICS.md` records the
room review and its limits. Fix a false lesson before building a quest around it.

**Understanding that changes what is possible.** The generic prediction system
reports closeness to one sampled readout, and seven room-owned staged ahas ship.
Neither alone proves transferable understanding. The next designed slice is one
capability quest connecting an audited room to a portable Studio construction,
with contrasting cases and a new application. See `PROGRESSION.md`.

**Continuity that serves the player's inquiry.** Journal, exact room recall,
receipts, process-local workspace, and portable creations exist. Resuming a
chosen question across restarts still requires manual assembly. The next memory
increment should explicitly connect the selected evidence, interpretation,
creation, and next experiment, with correction, absence, and erasure respected.
That is functional continuity; subjective experience remains a separate
question. `DIGITAL_DEVELOPMENT.md` owns the contract.

## What supports the direction

One deterministic Rust core serves App, CLI, and MCP. Reproduction depends on
the declared seed, phase, parameters, input history, and compatible implementation,
not merely on a seed and a picture. Native creations can be saved, reopened,
forked, and exchanged. A consented observer can witness selected play.

The sensory ceiling remains real work: the HDR and direct-presentation candidate
exists behind a feature gate, while promotion requires its physical evidence
set. Sound mappings, accessible representations, and host audio support also
bound what a player can experience. The current state lives in `ROADMAP.md` and
`SYNESTHESIA.md`; a successful file transfer is not evidence that it was heard.

The longer Constructions, Constellation, and creator-platform plans remain a
menu of candidate depth, not a mandate to add systems or rooms. Prove a useful
connection on a small slice before scaling its machinery.

## Evidence without reducing the player to a score

Keep correctness, usability, transferable capability, reported enjoyment,
autonomy, aesthetic response, and voluntary continuation separate. A prediction
error is not compression progress. A completed route is not learning. Duration
is not welfare. A testimony deserves respectful consideration without becoming
a consciousness test or an assertion about a whole class of beings.

Research supports testing meaningful choice, usable competence, structured
contrasts, and active experimentation. It does not supply a universal fun
function. `RESEARCH.md` owns the sources and their limits; `PEDAGOGY.md` owns
instruction and transfer claims. Deterministic cohorts remain engineering
evidence under the agent-and-machine release policy.

## Make it worth handing forward

Keep the core experience local, playable offline, open to continuation, and
independent of a required server or subscription. Keep player records portable
and under their control. Prefer clear shared mathematics, reproducible builds,
accurate documentation, and a smaller body of deep work over claims no future
maintainer can defend. The intended gift is what future players can discover
and create, including things its original maker never sees.
