## Work items

Counts overlap when a paper offers more than one possible destination. A link
is an opportunity assignment, not a commitment to implement the entire paper.

### object-readings

**Keep the object, expose the calculation.** Stage: foundation. Assigned manuscripts: 0.

- **First gesture:** Move a curve crossing or a route point and pin a comparison without leaving the scene.
- **Game:** Make two different-looking creations share a measured property, or improve a route and keep the witness.
- **Expert workflow:** Inspect values, units, domains, method status, resource limits and export replayable numeric data.
- **Core seam:** Studio creations and Route Lab saved state
- **Validation:** A saved object reopens with the same calculation inputs across App, CLI and MCP; unsupported cases are explicit.
- **Scope:** No second expression parser, persistence format owner, or general notebook.
- **Revisit or reject:** Reject a universal result schema that erases operation-specific evidence.
- **Depends on:** No proposed work-item prerequisite.

### graph-workbench

**Make a network, challenge its answer.** Stage: first-slice. Assigned manuscripts: 50.

- **First gesture:** Drag a town, close an edge or change a cost and see the route reorganize.
- **Game:** Design a tiny map that defeats a heuristic; trade a counterexample and repair it.
- **Expert workflow:** Compare exact bounded baselines with heuristics; later inspect degree-preserving moves, transitions and walk statistics.
- **Core seam:** Route Lab maps, route comparisons, later typed finite graphs
- **Validation:** Recompute cost and feasibility independently; round-trip the full graph including directedness and edge semantics.
- **Scope:** Route certificates first; scheduling and subgroup posets are different adapters, never relabeled road maps.
- **Revisit or reject:** Stop if imported graph semantics silently change or a heuristic gains an optimality label.
- **Depends on:** [object-readings](#object-readings).

### wave-code-instrument

**A wave becomes a message.** Stage: first-slice. Assigned manuscripts: 30.

- **First gesture:** Flip or drag coefficients and hear a declared timbre mapping while correlation peaks change.
- **Game:** Build a quiet sidelobe pattern or send a friend's drawing through a chosen noisy channel.
- **Expert workflow:** Export exact finite coefficients, correlation conventions, normalization and seeded channel trials.
- **Core seam:** Studio coefficient sequences, harmonic sound and future sequence analysis
- **Validation:** Check direct sums on tiny sequences, complex conjugation and boundary conventions; playback loudness remains bounded.
- **Scope:** A short sequence/correlation instrument first; communications capacity is a separate mathematical claim.
- **Revisit or reject:** Revise if audio is decoration or uncontrolled loudness masks the mathematical change.
- **Depends on:** [object-readings](#object-readings).

### curve-inspector

**A curve you can question.** Stage: first-slice. Assigned manuscripts: 35.

- **First gesture:** Drag a bracket around a moving zero; watch the crossing answer immediately.
- **Game:** Find parameters giving a chosen crossing, slope or area; ignore the challenge and draw freely.
- **Expert workflow:** Bracketed root finding and bounded parameter sweeps on the supported grammar, with endpoint/domain checks and sample export.
- **Core seam:** Existing Studio expression and slider objects
- **Validation:** Endpoint zeros, no sign change, discontinuities, non-finite values and cancellation have separate outcomes; brackets carry the continuity assumptions.
- **Scope:** One root operation first; fitting, integration and symbolic calculus each need a later contract.
- **Revisit or reject:** Stop if the same expression is interpreted differently by the plot and the calculator.
- **Depends on:** [object-readings](#object-readings).

### matrix-workbench

**Transformations with receipts.** Stage: shared-object. Assigned manuscripts: 118.

- **First gesture:** Pull a shape's axes or edit entries; follow the transformed grid and selected vectors.
- **Game:** Undo a deformation, preserve an area or construct two transformations that fail to commute.
- **Expert workflow:** Small real matrix products, solves and selected decompositions with residuals, conditioning and precision limits.
- **Core seam:** New bounded finite matrix object in core
- **Validation:** Verify exact small fixtures and independent residuals; handle singular/repeated/ill-conditioned inputs and preserve basis conventions.
- **Scope:** Small real matrices first; complex channels, sparse operators and semidefinite optimization are separate extensions.
- **Revisit or reject:** Reject a broad solver dependency until ownership, determinism and numerical failure behavior are explicit.
- **Depends on:** [object-readings](#object-readings).

### probability-instrument

**Make randomness inspectable.** Stage: shared-object. Assigned manuscripts: 127.

- **First gesture:** Reweight a handful of outcomes or move a transition and watch a repeatable ensemble respond.
- **Game:** Invent a fair guessing game or discover two mechanisms that look similar in small samples.
- **Expert workflow:** Compare exact finite expectations with seeded estimates, uncertainty summaries and mixing diagnostics under a declared sampling law.
- **Core seam:** Bounded finite distributions, seeded sampling and declared transition kernels
- **Validation:** Finite enumeration baselines, probability normalization, seed replay and explicit correlated-sample handling; no universal convergence claim from a short run.
- **Scope:** Finite distributions and one chosen walk first; continuum probability and inferred statistical models need separate contracts.
- **Revisit or reject:** Reject an unexplained sampling bias or a confidence label without its assumptions.
- **Depends on:** [object-readings](#object-readings).

### finite-topology

**Build a space and inspect its invariant.** Stage: shared-object. Assigned manuscripts: 12.

- **First gesture:** Snap cells together and remove a free face while cycles rearrange.
- **Game:** Build different shapes with equal checked homology, then discover what the invariant misses.
- **Expert workflow:** Compute bounded boundary operators and homology; inspect move certificates, subgroup chains and signed cancellations.
- **Core seam:** Finite complexes, coefficient domains and exact boundary matrices
- **Validation:** Check boundary squared equals zero, coefficient selection and explicit small complexes; reject malformed incidence data.
- **Scope:** Finite combinatorial objects first; smooth four-manifold and chromatic claims are specialist lenses.
- **Revisit or reject:** Stop if equal homology is presented as equivalent spaces or a planar knot models arbitrary four-dimensional geometry.
- **Depends on:** [object-readings](#object-readings), [matrix-workbench](#matrix-workbench).
