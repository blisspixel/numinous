## The mathematical question

The manuscript constructs one connected finite etale p-adic curve cover with a degree-one exterior component and degree divisible by p on every component over the prescribed disks; etale does not mean branching over the disks.

## Inside the construction

### exact-construction

Cut one skeletal edge at a type-three point, take p labelled copies, and glue outgoing to incoming labels by a -> a+1. Traversal creates transitive monodromy while the exterior avoids the cut.

- **Source locator:** lem:graph-character
- **Numinous use:** A graph-cover toy lets a player cut, thread, and follow luminous paths; an expert exports sheet permutations and connected components.
- **Limit:** This is the paper's graph stage, not an arbitrary complex branched-cover model; algebraization needs the stated analytic inputs.

### finite-witness

Endpoint torsors multiply edges faster than vertices: a lifted m-edge path has E=m*l and V<=(m-1)*l+2, hence positive first Betti number.

- **Source locator:** prop:cycles
- **Numinous use:** A graph surgery object can expose exact E-V+c alongside the emerging loops.
- **Limit:** Positive-genus residue components and existence of the geometric cover are additional inputs.

### group-computation

Quotienting by an exterior-component stabilizer H makes that sheet degree one; disk degrees are [B:B intersection H], divisible by p through a nontrivial normal p-subgroup disjoint from H.

- **Source locator:** eq:disjoint; eq:nontrivial
- **Numinous use:** A finite coset-action inspector connects local degrees to visible orbit counts.
