## The mathematical question

Clique-free graphs allegedly contain independent sets larger by a logarithmic degree factor than the basic scale.

## Visual and sonic possibility

## What makes it hard

The theorem uses average degree rather than maximum degree and fixed excluded clique size. A heuristic independent set certifies its size only, not optimality or the universal lower-bound constant.

## Inside the construction

### invariant

At a maximizer every vertex weight is positive and log(1/w_v) equals its weighted neighbor mass.

- **Source locator:** eq:stationarity
- **Numinous use:** Solve small graphs and inspect the balance at each vertex.
- **Limit:** A stationary point is not automatically a certified global maximizer.

### identity

The objective gap splits into coordinate relative-entropy terms plus the quadratic edge mass of the weight difference.

- **Source locator:** eq:optimizer-divergence
- **Numinous use:** Compare candidate weights with an exact symmetric optimum.
- **Limit:** The quadratic term can be negative, so separate positivity is not implied.
