### factorization

The fourth-order operator factors into two shifted second-order radial operators; its flat model is a radial biharmonic operator.

- **Source locator:** geo:operators
- **Numinous use:** Compare direct fourth derivatives with factorized evaluation and inspect conditioning.
- **Limit:** A stable numerical scheme does not prove the claimed finite-time blow-up.
