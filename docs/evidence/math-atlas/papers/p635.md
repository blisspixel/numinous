## The mathematical question

Midpoint lenses encode convexity through overlap of balls.

## Visual and sonic possibility

Proposed visual: Pull two points apart and watch the intersection of their metric balls narrow. No physical sound model is claimed.

## What makes it hard

Finite measurements cannot determine an asymptotic convexity modulus without bounds.

## Inside the construction

### algorithm

Choose an inward sign from the shortest head suffix crossing a threshold, then clip cumulative tail sums; coefficient increments remain controlled.

- **Source locator:** clip:crossing; clip:two-clips
- **Numinous use:** A tree signal editor can show cumulative sums and exact clipping changes.
- **Limit:** Cone and last-head grouping need their own valid disjointness budgets.

### certificate

Splitting an opposite-sign suffix pays for a large positive crossing term; this keeps squared cost quadratic rather than accumulating loss per level.

- **Source locator:** clip:split-gain
- **Numinous use:** Expose every split as a local arithmetic certificate and sum its budget.
- **Limit:** The final lens estimate allows displacement with a nonzero head; a tail-only demo omits that strength.
