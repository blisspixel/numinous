## What makes it hard

The abstract provides an existence bound, not a practical optimizer. A bounded screen cannot certify whole-space coverage without periodic fundamental-cell reasoning, and high-dimensional constants may be poor. The asymptotic lemma has large unspecified thresholds. Small toys demonstrate the averaging operator, not its quantitative guarantee. A finite union of good translated patterns need not be one lattice; the shear hierarchy is an additional construction.

## Inside the construction

### reusable-lemma

For a finite abelian group, repeated shifts average g over Boolean subset sums. The second stage upgrades almost-everywhere control to every base point through E(Phi_next)=Phi^2, without treating the subset-sum labels as independent.

- **Source locator:** lem:sampler; eq:sampler-potential-square
- **Numinous use:** A finite torus sampler lets players place heat, choose shift vectors, and watch holes fill; experts inspect exact minimum averages, collisions, and potential decay.
- **Limit:** The asymptotic lemma has large unspecified thresholds. Small toys demonstrate the averaging operator, not its quantitative guarantee.

### exact-factorization

A folded Gaussian separates into total weight F_b(t) and a product law on adjacent-integer corrections; preserving total mass and suppressing individual atoms are separate constraints.

- **Source locator:** eq:folded-identity; lem:folded-grid
- **Numinous use:** Expose a binary correction tree with branch weights and a mass ledger, reusable for probabilistic sampling beyond coverings.
- **Limit:** A finite union of good translated patterns need not be one lattice; the shear hierarchy is an additional construction.
