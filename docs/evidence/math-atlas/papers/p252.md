## The mathematical question

The claimed construction separates block sensitivity from the square of single-bit sensitivity for total Boolean functions.

## Visual and sonic possibility

Single edges and multi-bit jump ribbons share the cube; flips can drive mapped sounds.

## What makes it hard

A finite ratio above a chosen constant is not the superquadratic asymptotic separation. Block optimization becomes combinatorial quickly, and arbitrary Boolean examples do not reproduce the manuscript's actual construction.
