## The mathematical question

Uniform sparsest cut balances severed capacity against separated vertex pairs, with a claimed constant-factor hardness result.

## Visual and sonic possibility

Edge tension and island sizes encode the objective; ratio-to-pitch is mapped.

## What makes it hard

Uniform pair demand differs from arbitrary demand matrices. The hardness claim concerns growing instances, so keep exact small results usable and never present a visually small edge cut as automatically sparse.
