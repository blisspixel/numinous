## What makes it hard

Topological cycles and algebraic cycles are different objects. The consequences depend on deep arguments not inspected in this design survey. A nonzero numerical pairing alone does not construct an algebraic cycle. Algebraicity of these kernels relies on Hodge-theoretic hypotheses and descent, beyond finite tensor arithmetic.
