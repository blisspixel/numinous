## The mathematical question

The manuscript proves metric Markov cotype two for real l1 and constructs the required replacement points as expected coordinatewise medians of three independent geometric-walk endpoints.

## Visual and sonic possibility

Proposed visual: Assign outputs to a few points and try filling the missing point without stretching too much. No physical sound model is claimed.

## What makes it hard

The construction chooses new points y_i rather than prescribed linear averages. Finite computations can check the chosen instance; they do not verify the universal cotype theorem, and Monte Carlo output needs sampling error estimates.

## Inside the construction

### representation

Finite ell1 data split into nonnegative weighted binary cuts, with an explicit linear reconstruction; squared weighted-Hilbert distances equal binary ell1 distances.

- **Source locator:** lem:cuts
- **Numinous use:** Build an exact cut-coordinate view and reconstruct the original points.
- **Limit:** A naive all-subsets list is exponential; coordinate threshold cuts may be much smaller.

### algorithm

The output is the expected coordinatewise median of three independent endpoints of geometric-length walks of mean t; majority probability is 3h^2-2h^3.

- **Source locator:** eq:median; sec:proof
- **Numinous use:** Offer nonlinear graph smoothing beside ordinary diffusion on identical data.
- **Limit:** The constructed y points replace the original points; they are not prescribed linear averages.
