## The mathematical question

A Boolean Fourier construction allegedly makes summed linear coefficients exceed every constant multiple of square-root degree.

## Visual and sonic possibility

Cube patterns link to a coefficient spectrum; Boolean sequences can be audified or coefficients explicitly mapped.

## What makes it hard

Finite coefficient measurements show only their instance ratio, not an unbounded family. Signed sums differ from sums of absolute values, and numerical thresholding must not erase small exact Fourier coefficients.
