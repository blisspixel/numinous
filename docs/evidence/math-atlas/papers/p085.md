## What makes it hard

The manuscript explicitly does not claim semiampleness of the original adjoint. Constant curvature pictures would erase precisely this distinction. Currents are measure-valued and global descent additionally uses closedness and connected fibers. Numerical descent remains distinct from descent of an actual line bundle.
