## Inside the construction

### construction

Each weighted vertex becomes F_p to the sixth power; edges are selected by dot products in a residue interval of length floor(p times weight).

- **Source locator:** lem:unweighted
- **Numinous use:** Construct tiny field examples and compare weighted and unweighted cuts.
- **Limit:** The polynomial blowup can be enormous; loops are deleted without renormalizing weights.

### certificate

Finite-field character matrices have operator norm p cubed, controlling every rectangular edge discrepancy and hence arbitrary cuts splitting clusters.

- **Source locator:** eq:rectangle-discrepancy
- **Numinous use:** Show character orthogonality and exact rectangle discrepancies.
- **Limit:** Small illustrative primes need not meet the theorem's error bound.
