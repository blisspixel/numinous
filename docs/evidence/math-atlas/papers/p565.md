### computational-asset

The Forrelation scalar alternates diagonal sign matrices and normalized Walsh transforms between uniform vectors; its coherent acceptance probability is the square of that scalar.

### totalization-mechanism

An addressed accepting certificate verifies partial-function answers and supplies a zero witness for every other column, making the assembled function total even off promise.
