## The mathematical question

Ruled segment families become finite cylinder covers with controlled projection cost, including shear fields with square-zero derivatives.

## Visual and sonic possibility

Flowing segment bundles become tiled prisms; area-error tones are mapped rather than physical flow sound.

## What makes it hard

The C1 and strict eigenvalue hypotheses matter; arbitrary brush fields can violate them. A numerical mesh supports exploration but cannot prove convergence or coverage for every compact label set.

## Inside the construction

### algorithm

For 0<=alpha beta<1, nested nearest-integer rounding gives a monotone integer map whose bounded iteration finds a square covering a target point.

- **Source locator:** b:lattice
- **Numinous use:** Provide an exact target-to-cover-cell locator and visualize its iterations.
- **Limit:** The sign and product hypotheses are essential.

### representation

Any trace-zero 2 by 2 matrix admits an orthonormal zero-diagonal basis, with off-diagonal product minus determinant.

- **Source locator:** b:zero-diagonal
- **Numinous use:** Rotate a velocity derivative into shear coordinates and compute its cover parameters.
- **Limit:** The basis selection is separate from nonlinear freezing errors.
