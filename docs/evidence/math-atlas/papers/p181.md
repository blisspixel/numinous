## The mathematical question

The Coulomb result uses direct Voronoi-cell comparison for renormalized fields and connects planar order to spherical logarithmic energy.

## Visual and sonic possibility

Visual: shared particles, Voronoi cells, reciprocal-space peaks, and energy readings. Energy or defect tones are sonification, not a physical sound prediction.

## What makes it hard

Planar lattice order cannot tile a sphere without defects. The claimed asymptotic spherical coefficient needs its separate comparison theorem, not a visual transfer.

## Inside the construction

### geometric-series

Sixfold symmetry leaves only powers z^(6m) in the regular part of the triangular Green function. Polynomial edge data match those modes on the regular hexagon.

- **Source locator:** cal:green-series; cal:edge-polynomial; cal:continuation
- **Numinous use:** Paint the hexagonal potential mode by mode and expose exact edge matching versus auxiliary continuation.
- **Limit:** The continuation outside the regular hexagon is a test potential, not the same Green function; singularities and omitted modes need explicit handling.
