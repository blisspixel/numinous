## The mathematical question

Liouville signs along two different affine progressions are claimed to decorrelate in ordinary averages.

## Visual and sonic possibility

Visual: the concrete prerequisite or exact object described above, with optional numeric readings. Audio is optional sonification of declared quantities; no inherent sound is claimed.

## What makes it hard

Deterministic signs are not independent random coin tosses. Finite decay cannot establish every-cutoff bounds or nonpretentiousness hypotheses. Integer residues from a short interval are only approximately distributed like the product law. Independently resampling each translated site would change the model and invalidate the comparison.

## Inside the construction

### sampling-construction

Jittering a residue R_s by an independent U_s and encoding (R_s+U_s)/s in binary creates unbiased independent bits under the product residue law; ambiguous boundary cells have an explicit decoding error.

- **Source locator:** q:decoding-error

### dependence-boundary

Every translated test at a fixed modulus uses the same residue coordinate; Chinese remainder counting controls selected small marginals without averaging over the full enormous joint period.

- **Source locator:** q:finite-law-section; q:finite-law
