## The mathematical question

The complete-graph crossing manuscript claims the Harary-Hill formula for ordinary topological crossing number.

## Visual and sonic possibility

Thread-like edges make a beautiful manipulable knot of connections; crossings may trigger mapped notes.

## What makes it hard

Ordinary crossing number permits curved edges; straight-line minima are a different invariant. Handle tangencies, triple crossings, and shared endpoints explicitly, and do not infer optimality from one attractive drawing.

## Inside the construction

### construction

Combine signed interior crossings with half the oriented spoke-order contributions at shared endpoints to form a skew pairing J.

- **Source locator:** eq:J
- **Numinous use:** Edit a drawing and inspect both local and interior contributions.
- **Limit:** Independent edges may cross more than once.

### invariant

The pairing vanishes on cycle chains, giving exact consistency checks on drawing data.

- **Source locator:** lem:cycles
- **Numinous use:** Compare a cycle basis against the computed form.
- **Limit:** This identity does not by itself establish the complete-graph crossing formula.
