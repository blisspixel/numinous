## The mathematical question

Under the ordinary approximation property, finite cotype of a space and its dual characterizes K-convexity.

## Visual and sonic possibility

Proposed visual: Compare random signed sums of vectors in a normed space and its dual. No physical sound model is claimed.

## What makes it hard

Finite-dimensional estimates do not certify universal cotype or K-convexity; the approximation-property assumption is essential.

## Inside the construction

### finite-construction

Every binary r by s matrix is a pairing matrix of two independent frames in dimension r+s, using (e_i,0) and (column_j,e_j).

- **Source locator:** lem:frames
- **Numinous use:** An exact F2 frame editor can construct and transform pairing data.
- **Limit:** Independent frames on both sides are required.

### operator

The Rademacher projection keeps first-order sign coefficients; Walsh norm growth compares tests at nearly doubled dimension.

- **Source locator:** lem:growth
- **Numinous use:** Provide sign-cube transforms and norm experiments on finite-dimensional Banach models.
- **Limit:** Ordinary AP does not supply one uniform bound on approximating operator norms.
