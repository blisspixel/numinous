## The mathematical question

Rational homology of the poset of nontrivial elementary abelian p-subgroups detects a finite-group condition.

## What makes it hard

## Inside the construction

### construction

Flags in commuting elementary subgroups combine by signed lattice-path shuffles; the boundary satisfies an explicit Leibniz rule.

- **Source locator:** eq:chain-split-shuffle
- **Numinous use:** A subgroup-poset instrument can construct cycles and animate cancelling faces.
- **Limit:** Counting formal apartments does not establish independence of their actual chain images.

### certificate

The coordinate apartment sums permutation flags; its surviving boundary deletes one basis subgroup at a time.

- **Source locator:** eq:chain-decoration-boundary
- **Numinous use:** Export rational boundary matrices and nonboundary witnesses.
- **Limit:** A finite example illustrates the machinery, not the all-group reduction.
