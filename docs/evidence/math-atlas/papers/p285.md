## The mathematical question

The ergodicity companion concerns individual triangular billiard flow with at least one irrational angle and excludes vertex-hit trajectories.

## Visual and sonic possibility

Ray trails become woven textures; bounce sequences are mapped audio.

## What makes it hard

Dense-looking trails do not prove ergodicity. Vertex hits require an explicit policy, and this result does not automatically claim mixing or a quantitative equidistribution speed.

## Inside the construction

### representation

Forward and perpendicular horizontal derivatives are expressed through angular factors multiplying complex spatial derivatives.

- **Source locator:** eq:XY
- **Numinous use:** Compare motion along a ray with a transverse perturbation.
- **Limit:** The equations live on the regular tangent bundle.

### construction

Two equally weighted sheets fold back to the original billiard measure, and regular time maps permute sheet choices.

- **Source locator:** sec:geometry
- **Numinous use:** Follow sheet state and position-direction measure together.
- **Limit:** Irrational-angle assumptions enter later; this geometry works for any nondegenerate triangle.
