## What makes it hard

## Inside the construction

### invariant

Image distinction ignores starting point, orientation and iteration; even a round sphere has infinitely many prime images of the same length.

- **Source locator:** thm:spheres
- **Numinous use:** A geodesic explorer must deduplicate by geometric image rather than period or initial state alone.
- **Limit:** Finite numerical trajectories cannot establish infinitude.

### proof-mechanism

Product powers raise degree while a cut operation removes a long loop segment; resonance makes the remaining length allowance independent of the power.

- **Source locator:** eq:cj-fixed-difference
- **Numinous use:** A loop-operation tool can track degree, concatenation and length budgets separately.
- **Limit:** The chain-level cut-join identity is not the same as visually cutting one drawn loop.
