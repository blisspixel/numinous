## The mathematical question

The low-space companion trades time for about two-to-the-n-over-five writable words with one-sided error.

## What makes it hard

Rejection may miss a solution; acceptance must certify one. Word widths matter, so exponent-only memory is neither a byte count nor constant space, and ordinary writable storage excludes special hardware assumptions.
