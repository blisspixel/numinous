### exact-calculation

Integer spectral cuts approximate tau(c) by the sum of their ranks, with error at most the support rank d_tau(c), independent of the number of spectral levels.

### construction

Trace transfer rewrites r(a) as sum z_i* z_i and moves the same trace to sum z_i z_i* in a prescribed hereditary support.

- **Source locator:** ext:lem:transfer
