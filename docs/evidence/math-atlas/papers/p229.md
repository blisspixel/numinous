## The mathematical question

The characteristic-zero companion claims one rational matrix tuple detects every nonzero bounded-size division-free noncommutative formula.

## What makes it hard

## Inside the construction

### construction

The operator integrating a(t)/(t+i) has jet matrix entry (-1)^(r-q-1)/(r i^(r-q)) below the diagonal.

- **Source locator:** eq:explicit-matrices
- **Numinous use:** Compose letter matrices and inspect exact rational jets.
- **Limit:** Characteristic zero is essential for these denominators.

### certificate

Evaluating a noncommutative polynomial on the matrices and applying it to the constant column recovers its encoded jet, including constant terms.

- **Source locator:** eq:first-column
- **Numinous use:** Compare symbolic word expansions with matrix evaluation.
- **Limit:** The detection dimension depends on the stated formula-size bound.
