## The mathematical question

The k-server existence theorem bounds online movement on arbitrary metrics against request sequences fixed independently of algorithmic randomness.

## What makes it hard

The existence theorem does not automatically give an executable policy. Requests adapted to revealed random choices violate the oblivious-adversary hypothesis, so mark free improvisation separately from guaranteed benchmark runs.

## Inside the construction

### construction

Clip old integer coordinates into new floor-ceiling intervals, adjust their sum to the realized total, then repair means through total-preserving exchanges.

- **Source locator:** lem:rounding-star
- **Numinous use:** Round moving fractional allocations without changing the total in any outcome.
- **Limit:** Coordinate means are unconditional over the joint state and rounding kernel.

### certificate

The local kernel bounds expected movement by 32 times fractional movement plus 11 times expected total change.

- **Source locator:** eq:rounding-star-bound
- **Numinous use:** Compare exact finite-law movement against the stated bound.
- **Limit:** Conditioning additionally on rounded ancestors need not preserve the same means.
