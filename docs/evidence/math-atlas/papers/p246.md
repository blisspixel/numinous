## The mathematical question

The superstring algorithm promises a factor-two bound through a construction distinct from classical maximum-overlap greedy.

## Visual and sonic possibility

Overlapping ribbons form a continuous string; symbol sequences may be mapped to melody.

## What makes it hard

A superstring must contain every original word, including cases with contained or repeated strings. Do not apply the guarantee to a familiar greedy animation merely because it also merges maximum overlaps.
