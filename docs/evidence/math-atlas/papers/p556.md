## Inside the construction

### exact-ensemble

The equiprobable states sqrt(1-p)|0> +/- sqrt(p)|1> have equal output entropy; opposite coherences cancel in their average, leaving a one-parameter attainable Holevo objective.

- **Source locator:** eq:pair; eq:holevo-upper
- **Numinous use:** Show two signals, their noisy outputs, and the entropy gap of their average in a channel bench.
- **Limit:** This is Holevo information and classical capacity, not coherent information or quantum capacity.

### optimization-structure

A population-dependent entropy bound and convexity establish the ensemble upper bound; minimum output entropy alone would not suffice.
