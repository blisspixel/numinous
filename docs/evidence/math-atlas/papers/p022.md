## The mathematical question

Prime-predecessor factor sizes are claimed to approach a Poisson-Dirichlet partition law.

## Visual and sonic possibility

Visual: exact arithmetic objects with linked distributions or construction traces. Any factor, residue, or event-to-note mapping is optional sonification with a documented rule.

## What makes it hard

The claim is finite-joint convergence of ranked shares, not literal randomness of prime factorization. Repeated factors must retain multiplicity. Resampling the line at every visit would erase the dependence central to the construction. The finite sampler does not establish the determinant moment or Poisson-Dirichlet limit.

## Inside the construction

### normalization

Endpoint visits use sqrt(q) while interior visits use q; the adjusted prime measure differs from the original by a controlled factor depending on path length over p.
