## Inside the construction

### representation

A complete graph carries symmetric nonzero off-diagonal lattice labels obeying the join-triangle inequality; admissible self-maps decrease those labels.

- **Source locator:** f:def-placement; f:graph-triangle
- **Numinous use:** Paint edges with lattice elements and immediately locate violated join-triangle constraints.
- **Limit:** A valid labelled graph alone is not the complete representation criterion.

### certificate

For each nonempty edge set, close its images under all admissible self-maps and test the required connectivity, together with faithful order-reflection witnesses.

- **Source locator:** f:criterion; f:graph-connectivity
- **Numinous use:** Turn the representation question at a fixed vertex count into exact finite closure and connectivity checks.
- **Limit:** Searching all vertex counts has no termination guarantee supplied by this criterion.
