## What makes it hard

The prime congruence conclusion concerns rational cubes, not integer cubes. Bounded searches may miss very large-height points and cannot verify the general converse. Searching bounded-height points is not a decision procedure for rational solvability. A rank bound alone cannot replace the Selmer-corank hypothesis of the converse.
