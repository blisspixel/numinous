## Inside the construction

### transformation

A positive supersolution v of (-4 Delta+Scal)v>=v gives t=-2 log v and weighted scalar D(g,t)>=1.

- **Source locator:** eq:log-weight
- **Numinous use:** A spectral tool can turn a positive eigenfunction into a density and compare the identity numerically.
- **Limit:** On noncompact spaces the source uses exhaustion and local limits, not a single global eigensolve.

### geometry

The theorem gives a graph map with entire fibers bounded by 500/sqrt(lambda), including distances between disconnected components.

- **Source locator:** thm:main
- **Numinous use:** A graph-collapse viewer should inspect complete fibers, not only connected level sets.
- **Limit:** The constant is not optimized, and an arbitrary Reeb graph is not the asserted map.
