## The mathematical question

Integral and fractional expectation thresholds allegedly agree within a universal constant under the same covering budget.

## Visual and sonic possibility

## What makes it hard

The actual stated comparison constant is 25 times 512^4, with covering budget one-half on both sides. This is structural equivalence, not a tight finite-instance prediction or evidence that a fractional solution is nearly integral in practice.
