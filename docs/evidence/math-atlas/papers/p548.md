## What makes it hard

## Inside the construction

### representation-mechanism

Conditioned on total spin J, one component is uniform on -J through J. Concentration of J/V at m turns the discrete transform into sinh(m|t|)/(m|t|), the spherical law.

- **Source locator:** eq:casimir; eq:spin-concentration
- **Numinous use:** Link a sphere, exact component histogram, and transform on one spin-sector object.
- **Limit:** Finite noncommuting spin components do not possess a joint classical measurement distribution.

### diagnostic

- **Source locator:** thm:main; prop:global-second-moment
