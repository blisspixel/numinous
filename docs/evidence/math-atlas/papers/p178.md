## The mathematical question

Gaussian Fourier minorants are claimed to certify triangular-lattice optimality for all nonnegative completely monotone squared-distance potentials.

## Visual and sonic possibility

Visual: shared particles, Voronoi cells, reciprocal-space peaks, and energy readings. Energy or defect tones are sonification, not a physical sound prediction.

## What makes it hard

Finite relaxation can get trapped and is not global optimality. The theorem allows infinite energies and does not classify all minimizers.

## Inside the construction

### duality-mechanism

For the covolume-one triangular lattice, the reciprocal lattice is a quarter-turn. Gaussian Fourier duality pairs alpha with 1/alpha and an essential alpha^-1 factor.

- **Source locator:** energy:reciprocal; energy:poisson
- **Numinous use:** Couple a lattice energy view to its reciprocal-space view with explicit scale and omitted-tail bounds.
- **Limit:** Finite lattice sums approximate the infinite energy; the full optimality claim additionally depends on the manuscript's interpolation and sign certificate.

### comparison-ledger

The Fourier comparison removes exactly one diagonal contribution per point before passing to a density limit; positive Laplace mixtures extend Gaussian comparisons to completely monotone potentials.

- **Source locator:** energy:lp; energy:lp-bound; energy:laplace-representation
- **Numinous use:** Let an expert combine nonnegative Gaussian kernels and inspect pair counting, self-energy removal and truncation separately.
- **Limit:** A finite point cloud is not an asymptotic density witness, and arbitrary signed kernel mixtures do not inherit the argument.
