## Inside the construction

### construction

A common basis A corresponds to the transversal selecting x_i for i in A and y_i otherwise in M1 direct-sum dual(M2).

- **Source locator:** sec:reduction
- **Numinous use:** Toggle paired choices and compare both rank tests.
- **Limit:** Exact independence oracles and matroid axioms are hypotheses.

### representation

The positive weight q^(n-rank(S)) softens rank failure and includes one-empty-one-full pair defect states.

- **Source locator:** eq:rankweights
- **Numinous use:** Lower temperature and watch deficient states lose weight.
- **Limit:** This representation alone does not establish mixing or approximate counting.
