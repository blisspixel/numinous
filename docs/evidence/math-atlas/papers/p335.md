## The mathematical question

Hypergraph properties receive a uniform influence bound under biased product measure, even without monotonicity.

## Visual and sonic possibility

## What makes it hard

Vertex-relabeling invariance and fixed uniformity are essential. Nonmonotone influence statements do not automatically yield monotone threshold-width conclusions; keep these two uses separate.

## Inside the construction

### construction

Keep hyperedges meeting a chosen vertex block free and condition on all other edge bits.

- **Source locator:** sec:restriction
- **Numinous use:** Freeze exterior edges while permuting the selected block.
- **Limit:** This is not restriction to the induced subhypergraph alone.

### invariant

A free edge with j block vertices has orbit size binomial(m,j), at least m, making individual influences small relative to the total.

- **Source locator:** lem:block
- **Numinous use:** Compare exact orbit sizes and conditional influences.
- **Limit:** The averaged Fourier bound assumes a vertex-permutation-invariant property.
