## Inside the construction

### construction

Multiply old vectors by positive mean-one variables that are small with high probability; individual products have little overlap, while their average recovers the old vector.

- **Source locator:** kw:enlargement
- **Numinous use:** A probability-signal instrument can compare rare spikes, L1 overlap and averaging.
- **Limit:** Finite sampling needs tail-error control; rare events can dominate L1.

### invariant

For the specified measure-precompact Daugavet space, midpoint and one-sided moduli are respectively max(t/2,t-1) and max(0,t-2).

- **Source locator:** thm:exact
- **Numinous use:** Plot both exact profiles and compare sampled finite enlargements.
- **Limit:** These formulas depend on both stated infinite-dimensional hypotheses.
