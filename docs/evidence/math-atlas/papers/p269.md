## The mathematical question

Projection moments and spherical-cap domination provide three approaches to memory-sample lower bounds.

## Visual and sonic possibility

Overlapping caps and density strips are the object; moments map to pitch.

## What makes it hard

A finite picture cannot establish countable cap domination. The three proofs have distinct stopping and accuracy costs, so their guarantees should not collapse into a single undifferentiated bound.

## Inside the construction

### representation

Posterior-state measures are bounded by positive sums of normalized spherical caps, charging both mass and inverse radius.

- **Source locator:** p:cap-section
- **Numinous use:** Compare a density with a positive cap cover.
- **Limit:** Pointwise domination is stronger than fitting sampled values.

### construction

Factor a full-row-rank Gaussian matrix as H P with H=(XX transpose)^(1/2) and P having orthonormal rows.

- **Source locator:** p:cap-section
- **Numinous use:** Separate observation scaling from projection direction.
- **Limit:** Full row rank holds almost surely, not for every arbitrary input matrix.
