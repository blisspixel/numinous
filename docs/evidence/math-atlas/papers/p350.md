## Inside the construction

### algorithm

The compression product has form P+N with N strictly lower triangular, PN=NP=N and N^m=0; Q=P+sum_{k=1}^{m-1}(-N)^k gives Q(P+N)=P.

- **Source locator:** op:compression
- **Numinous use:** Expand the finite inverse correction and watch every off-diagonal term cancel.
- **Limit:** The corner identity uses P rather than assuming the ambient identity is P.

### certificate

Ordered annihilation F_ij P_i P_j=0 for i<j is enough for the construction; pairwise orthogonality or unrestricted commutativity is not assumed.

- **Source locator:** op:compression
