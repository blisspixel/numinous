## What makes it hard

## Inside the construction

### representation

A tree product P_v has mean one; conditioning on an ancestral finite head sends it exactly to the product at its last ancestor in that head.

- **Source locator:** prob:head-projection
- **Numinous use:** A product-tree instrument can compute conditional projections symbolically.
- **Limit:** Fresh multipliers must be independent with the specified mean.

### calculation

For real L1 functions the midpoint excess equals E(t abs(y)-abs(x)) positive-part, reducing a geometric question to a scalar tail integral.

- **Source locator:** prob:midpoint-identity
- **Numinous use:** Display the portions of a distribution that contribute to midpoint growth.
- **Limit:** AMUC is proved for exponential and Gaussian-square laws, not every positive mean-one multiplier.
