## Inside the construction

### identity

Adding N-squared df tensor df to the metric and subtracting log L from the weight preserves weighted volume exactly.

- **Source locator:** eq:graph-basic-bounds
- **Numinous use:** A metric deformation tool can retain a density while altering directional lengths.
- **Limit:** Preserving weighted volume is not preserving ordinary volume.

### energy

Weighted scalar change equals D^2-2U(D) plus two nonnegative terms, exposing the drift term that must be controlled.

- **Source locator:** lem:graph-identity
- **Numinous use:** Plot local terms separately and check the symbolic identity on explicit metrics.
- **Limit:** Arbitrary graph deformation need not improve weighted scalar curvature.
