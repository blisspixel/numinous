## What makes it hard

The thermodynamic low-temperature statement need not describe a tiny spin grid. Unweighted exchanges or independent site colors sample the wrong law. The order is volume, field to zero, then temperature to zero, with interaction and spin fixed.

## Inside the construction

### exact-finite-model

Poisson exchanges between 2S slots per site and seam permutations form cycles. Weighting pictures by 2^(cycle count), then coloring cycles fairly, gives the finite Gibbs magnetization law.

- **Source locator:** prop:loop-representation
- **Numinous use:** A woven exchange-ribbon object can be checked against tiny exact spin Hamiltonians.
- **Limit:** Unweighted exchanges or independent site colors sample the wrong law.

### asymptotic-mechanism

The full jump kernel determines the diffusion matrix and coefficient through det(D)^(-1/2); a selected sublattice is used only for rough estimates.

- **Source locator:** thm:main; How returns determine the coefficient
- **Numinous use:** Vary anisotropic bonds and compare dispersion, return sums, and the Bloch asymptote.
