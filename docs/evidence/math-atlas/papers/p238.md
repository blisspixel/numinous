## The mathematical question

The reconstruction algorithm recovers a binary word from independent deletion traces with known length and retention probability.

## Visual and sonic possibility

Missing beads leave gaps between aligned traces; surviving-symbol sound is an explicit mapping.

## What makes it hard

Deletion locations are unobserved, unlike ordinary masked-data recovery. Quasipolynomial bounds can be impractical, and finite posterior guesses should not be labeled certified exact reconstruction without the stated probabilistic contract.

## Inside the construction

### representation
