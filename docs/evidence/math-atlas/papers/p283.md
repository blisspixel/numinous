## The mathematical question

The companion claims boundedness and persistence for each positive trajectory, with bounds allowed to depend on its initial condition.

## Visual and sonic possibility

Species jars and orbit trails share the evolving state; concentration sound is mapped.

## What makes it hard

Per-trajectory bounds differ from uniform permanence across a class. Numerical underflow can imitate extinction, and a long bounded run proves neither all-time boundedness nor persistence.
