## Inside the construction

### proof-mechanism

A manifold model in degree two but no models in sufficiently large odd cyclic covers obstructs realizing a specified outer automorphism by a homeomorphism.

- **Source locator:** cyc:criterion
- **Numinous use:** A cover diagram could retain deck groups, conjugation and model-existence labels.
- **Limit:** The finite diagram is not an algorithm deciding manifold realizability.

### construction

A hypothetical lifted homeomorphism commutes with deck translation; adding a sufficiently large translation would produce the forbidden odd-cover models.

- **Source locator:** cyc:commutation
- **Numinous use:** Animate translation on a cyclic-cover strip and track induced subgroup indices.
- **Limit:** A strip is a low-dimensional analogy, not the four-dimensional chamber.
