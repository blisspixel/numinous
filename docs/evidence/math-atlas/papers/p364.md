## Inside the construction

### construction

Chains and tests are inserted into specified forward and reverse intervals of the original Boolean lattice; original meets and joins are preserved although its atoms need not remain atoms.

- **Source locator:** lat:boolean-forward; lat:boolean-reverse
- **Numinous use:** Build lattice extensions while checking the original sublattice table.
- **Limit:** Atomicity and rank are not preserved merely because meets and joins are.

### construction

Lower and upper detectors are horizontal sums of two chains glued at a shared element; their lengths are chosen from an earlier absolute bound before selecting a representing group.

- **Source locator:** lat:detector-lower; lat:detector-upper
- **Numinous use:** Offer reusable detector components with explicit order and meet/join tables.
- **Limit:** The reviewed construction does not supply a small numerical counterexample or a practical global search bound.
