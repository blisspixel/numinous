## The mathematical question

Tensor saturation for even spin groups claims invariant existence descends from common dilation when weights satisfy the root-lattice condition.

## Visual and sonic possibility

Weight polytopes and scaled lattice points are tangible; multiplicities can map to harmony explicitly.

## What makes it hard

Saturation concerns existence, not unchanged multiplicity. Dominance, rank at least two, and the root-lattice sum condition are essential; arbitrary Euclidean triangles do not encode the representation problem.

## Inside the construction

### representation

Type-D roots are plus or minus e_i plus or minus e_j; root-lattice parity and weight-lattice coordinates must be distinguished when displaying admissible endpoints.

- **Source locator:** sec:real-schedules
- **Numinous use:** Show reflection chambers with separate root- and weight-lattice overlays.
- **Limit:** The initial real correspondence does not assume the later integrality conditions.

### construction

A schedule follows a Weyl-transformed velocity and switches frames by root reflections subject to nonnegative position gaps and sign constraints; simultaneous switch order is part of the data.

- **Source locator:** def:schedule; real:signs
- **Numinous use:** Drag a path and insert legal reflection events with live constraint checks.
- **Limit:** An arbitrary bouncing path is not an admissible schedule.
