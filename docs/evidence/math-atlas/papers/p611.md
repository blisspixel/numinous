## The mathematical question

The shortest subnormal chain with cyclic quotients measures a reported chromatic loss.

## Visual and sonic possibility

Proposed visual: Find a shortest admissible route from a subgroup to its finite p-group. No physical sound model is claimed.

## What makes it hard

Arbitrary subgroup-chain distance is not the invariant; each inclusion must be normal with cyclic quotient.

## Inside the construction

### algorithm

Cyclic length is a shortest path through normal inclusions with cyclic quotients; quotient order p^2 can still cost one step.

- **Source locator:** thm:main
- **Numinous use:** Compute the invariant exactly in small subgroup lattices and expose optimal chains.
- **Limit:** The chromatic interpretation is a manuscript theorem, not established by the graph search.

### representation

Rational Burnside marks split cochain objects into subgroup-indexed idempotent pieces.

- **Source locator:** eq:cochain-products
- **Numinous use:** Add a table-of-marks view and exact idempotent calculations.
- **Limit:** Categorical rationalization differs from ordinary rationalization of spectra.
