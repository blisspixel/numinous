## The mathematical question

An almost-full-measure compact set is claimed able to exclude every affine copy of one fixed geometric sequence.

## Visual and sonic possibility

Visual: freely editable geometric configurations with scale and projection made explicit. Contact or coverage-event rhythms are optional sonification, never evidence of continuum behavior.

## What makes it hard

Excluding finite prefixes differs from excluding the infinite sequence. The constructed set depends on q and does not exclude all ratios simultaneously.

- **Limit:** The global deduction assumes the periodic hitting construction; drawing a few removed intervals does not realize the theorem.
