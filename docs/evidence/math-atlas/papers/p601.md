## Inside the construction

### invariant

The reduction leaves opposite slopes -2 and 2 on genus-two knots; the alleged identification is arbitrary and need not preserve the knot exterior.

- **Source locator:** prop:introduction:reduction
- **Numinous use:** Let users manipulate primitive torus slope pairs while separating exterior symmetries from filled-manifold comparisons.
- **Limit:** Simple surgery invariants do not prove the remaining case.

### finite-construction

Negative cells enlarge diag(-2,-2) by an index-two lattice to diag(-1,-1). Positive cells have matrix [[2,1],[1,0]] direct-summed with -I4 and an explicit three-sphere plumbing.

- **Source locator:** prop:stack:lattice
- **Numinous use:** An exact lattice board can calculate determinants, signatures, discriminant classes and blowdown basis changes.
- **Limit:** The lattice computation is only one part of the Floer and analytic stack argument.
