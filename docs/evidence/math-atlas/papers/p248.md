## The mathematical question

Ordered products of binary relations define one-way liveness, with a claimed exponential cost for deterministic two-way simulation.

## Visual and sonic possibility

Layered paths make nondeterminism visible without probability; path-triggered notes are mapped.

## What makes it hard

This paper concerns deterministic simulation rather than complementation. Growing alphabets and nonaccepting infinite computations matter; bounded tape animations cannot silently decide all looping behavior.
