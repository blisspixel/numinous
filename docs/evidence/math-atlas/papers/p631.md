## Inside the construction

### algorithm

For diameter-one K, R_n(a)=a/n+(1-1/n)T R_n(a) is computed by a strict contraction; its fixed-point defect is at most 1/n.

- **Source locator:** lem:resolvents
- **Numinous use:** An iteration instrument can compare original iteration with anchored contraction and display a residual certificate.
- **Limit:** Convergence of the inner contraction does not give a universal rate for the theorem's final fixed point.

### bound

Finite compositions and convex combinations of resolvents remain nonexpansive; displacement constants add under composition.

- **Source locator:** eq:map-displacement
- **Numinous use:** Let users compose operations while the engine propagates certified bounds.
- **Limit:** The later compactness and tree argument is not replaced by this finite calculus.
