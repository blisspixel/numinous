## Inside the construction

### optimization

For T=f(A), minimize tau subject to I<=H<=tau I and T*HT<=H; sqrt(H) turns T into a contraction and records the similarity condition number.

- **Source locator:** eq:minimum; eq:optimal-similarity
- **Numinous use:** Offer a small semidefinite metric solver with primal residuals and exported H.
- **Limit:** The bound tau<=4 needs the numerical-range/conformal hypotheses; it is not true for every stable matrix.

### certificate

The resolvent density has congruence I-D*D, making positivity explicit, and its moments recover D powers. One density serves all coefficient sizes.

- **Source locator:** prop:density
- **Numinous use:** Display positive matrix-valued boundary weights and reconstruct polynomial evaluations by quadrature.
- **Limit:** Numerical quadrature and approximate PSD tests require error bounds.
