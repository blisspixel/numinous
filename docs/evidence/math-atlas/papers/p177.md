## The mathematical question

Bounded treewidth gives a distortion bound depending on bag size, independent of graph size and edge-length ratios.

## Visual and sonic possibility

Visual: editable graph, highlighted paths, and exact distance tables. Route-event rhythm is optional sonification; graph distance retains its declared mathematical units.

## What makes it hard

The stated source uses bags of size at most k, corresponding to treewidth at most k minus one. This convention must be preserved.

## Inside the construction

### probability-construction

Equal-mass cut measures on tree-decomposition bags glue when their separator marginals agree. The global law factors by bag masses divided by separator masses, with zero-mass assignments handled separately.

- **Source locator:** lem:gluing; eq:gluing-product
- **Numinous use:** Edit small local probability tables and show why inconsistent overlaps prevent a global joint law.
- **Limit:** Enumerating the global table is exponential; bounded bags support factorized inspection, not unrestricted cheap enumeration.

### metric-construction

The measure of cuts separating a pair defines an L1 metric; an edgewise upper bound extends to every pair through shortest paths.

- **Source locator:** eq:cut-metric; eq:edge-extension
- **Numinous use:** Carry the same cut law into a graph-distance comparison and inspect where the bound is tight.
- **Limit:** Positive edge lengths and the decomposition's bag-size convention must remain explicit; this is not a guarantee for arbitrary local tables.
