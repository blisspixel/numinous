## Inside the construction

### algorithm

Plant a source set into a Bernoulli sample, resample a target with weight nu_b rho^(-|A_b|), and retain its portion outside the sample.

- **Source locator:** eq:posterior
- **Numinous use:** Compare source, target, and leftover fragment.
- **Limit:** At a fixed sample W, the target need not have its original marginal law.

### invariant

The retained fragment lies in both source and target, and adding it back to W covers the target.

- **Source locator:** eq:two-inclusions
- **Numinous use:** Verify both inclusions for every sampled transition.
- **Limit:** Spread assumptions govern quantitative failure estimates later.
