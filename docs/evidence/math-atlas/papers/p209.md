## Inside the construction

### construction

Split each vertex into a Min-controlled entry and an original-owner exit; a reset edge returns to the start with weight equal to the initial credit.

- **Source locator:** eq:energy-reset-equivalence
- **Numinous use:** Transform an energy game into a mean-payoff game visibly.

### certificate

A prefix whose weight is below minus the credit becomes a negative closed walk after reset, contradicting a positional mean-payoff winning policy.

- **Source locator:** eq:energy-reset-equivalence
