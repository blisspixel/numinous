## The mathematical question

Annular r-variation controls how triangular Hilbert outputs oscillate across scales, a stronger view than their largest value.

## Visual and sonic possibility

## What makes it hard

A fixed scale partition underestimates the supremum over point-dependent partitions. The estimate requires r greater than two and a continuum principal-value limit.

## Inside the construction

### matrix-calculus

The energy tr(P^(3/2)) has an explicit Hessian on supported Hermitian directions, with entry weights 3/(2(sqrt(p_i)+sqrt(p_j))) in an eigenbasis.

- **Source locator:** eq:hessian-metric
- **Numinous use:** Provide a positive-matrix energy landscape with exact small fixtures and numerical directional-curvature readings.
- **Limit:** Kernel directions require the stated support restriction; dividing by zero or ignoring rank changes would alter the calculation.

### representation

Three incident row stars expose matching nonzero Gram spectra and quadratic costs for a cyclic matrix product.

- **Source locator:** eq:row-star; eq:star-grams
- **Numinous use:** Let a player change one matrix edge in a triangle and follow the adjacent Gram spectra and energy costs.
- **Limit:** The finite matrix identities do not by themselves establish the singular-integral variation bound.
