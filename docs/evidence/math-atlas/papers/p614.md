## Inside the construction

### construction

A sieve is the zero fiber of a functor to the directed interval; a cosieve is its one fiber. These fibers preserve colimits via explicit adjoints.

- **Source locator:** lem:fibers
- **Numinous use:** A category editor could label objects and reject arrows incompatible with a sieve.
- **Limit:** A general full subcategory lacks this property.

### representation

Initial and final oriental segments are full subcategories, providing controlled pieces even when attachments identify objects.

- **Source locator:** lem:oriental-segments
- **Numinous use:** Show a pushout with tracked labels and recompute its nerve in low dimensions.
- **Limit:** Strict higher categories here permit noninvertible cells; they are not strict infinity-groupoids.
