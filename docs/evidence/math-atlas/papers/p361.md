## The mathematical question

Universal tensor squares allegedly contain every irreducible representation for symmetric groups except degrees two, four, and nine.

## What makes it hard

## Inside the construction

### algorithm

Kronecker multiplicities are computed from the character inner product sum chi_lambda(rho)^2 chi_nu(rho)/z_rho, with rim-hook recursion providing signed character values.

- **Source locator:** eq:finite-character
- **Numinous use:** Build and remove rim hooks while showing their leg signs and accumulated character values.
- **Limit:** This is an exact finite calculation, not a claim that the provided program was executed.

### certificate

For the stated ranks through 64, primes larger than 64 make all class denominators invertible; a nonzero residue certifies positivity of the integer multiplicity, while a zero residue is inconclusive.

- **Source locator:** prop:finite-check
- **Numinous use:** Use modular residues as compact positivity certificates and preserve inconclusive outcomes.
- **Limit:** The finite check excludes the stated exceptional ranks and does not prove all ranks.
