## Inside the construction

### random-convex-body

Sample a Poisson number of uniform sphere normals and intersect their symmetric slabs with a ball. A buffered body absorbs rounding of proposed covering centers.

- **Source locator:** eq:poisson-measure; eq:body; eq:buffered-body
- **Numinous use:** In small dimensions, cut a symmetric body with sampled slabs and test explicit finite covering patterns for holes.
- **Limit:** The asymptotic construction may require enormous intensity and dimension; a small visual sample does not witness the claimed n log n covering obstruction.

### encoding-boundary

Strict hole and volume margins survive sufficiently small perturbations, allowing rational normal lists; the manuscript explicitly does not provide a decision algorithm or an effective first dimension.

- **Source locator:** cor:rational-polytope; prop:rational; eq:volume-continuity
- **Numinous use:** Preserve rational normals and finite hole witnesses in an exported experiment, with stability margins.
- **Limit:** An existential first admissible rational list is not an implementable search guarantee, and the ball-truncated rational-normal body need not be a polytope.
