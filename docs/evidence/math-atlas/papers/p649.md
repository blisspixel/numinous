## Inside the construction

### calculation

The model profile is obtained by inverting the radial volume integral and evaluating sphere area; Euclidean and hyperbolic cases share one parameterized formula.

- **Source locator:** eq:model-area-volume
- **Numinous use:** Offer a volume-area calculator and compare deformable regions with the model curve.
- **Limit:** A comparison plot does not verify a general manifold curvature bound.

### constraint

Confinement counts all ambient perimeter, including contact with the confining sphere; ignoring that boundary changes the optimization problem.

- **Source locator:** eq:confined-profile
- **Numinous use:** Mark free and contact boundaries separately in a constrained shape optimizer.
- **Limit:** Equality rigidity concerns bounded positive-volume sets and determines the equality region, not exterior flatness.
