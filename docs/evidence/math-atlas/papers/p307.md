## The mathematical question

The higher-dimensional distinct-distance manuscript claims n-point sets force order n^(2/d) distinct distances for fixed d at least three.

## Visual and sonic possibility

Linked point clouds and distance-spectrum bars are faithful; distance values can map to tones.

## What makes it hard

Floating-point clustering can invent equal distances. Dimension-dependent constants and asymptotic lower bounds are separate from finite exact counts; a projected cloud must not supply the distance calculation.

## Inside the construction

### construction

The degree-D envelope is the common zero set of all degree-at-most-D polynomials vanishing on a set; a finite basis suffices.

- **Source locator:** poly:section
- **Numinous use:** Fit exact vanishing polynomials to point configurations.
- **Limit:** Approximate numerical nullspaces need certification.

### invariant

The envelope has the same degree-D restriction rank as the original set.

- **Source locator:** poly:envelope-equality
- **Numinous use:** Compare evaluation matrices before and after algebraic completion.
- **Limit:** Reduced component degrees and local intersection multiplicities are distinct quantities.
