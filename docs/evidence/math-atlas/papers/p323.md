## The mathematical question

Bounded-degree simplicial complexes allegedly expand coboundaries uniformly in every lower degree, extending graph expansion to higher dimensions.

## Visual and sonic possibility

Colored simplices reveal cancellation and boundary growth; parity events may map to sound.

## What makes it hard

Higher-dimensional complexes cannot be faithfully judged from projected geometry. Bounded vertex degree and exact normalization matter; finite sampling of cochains gives no certified global expansion minimum.

## Inside the construction

### construction

Reduce polynomial matrices modulo t^m, form tagged cosets of subgroups generated by all but one elementary generator, then retain the d-skeleton.

- **Source locator:** eq:construction
- **Numinous use:** Build a small coset complex from exact matrix arithmetic.
- **Limit:** The theorem requires sufficiently large odd field size.

### invariant

A simplex requires a common intersection of all its cosets, not merely pairwise intersections.

- **Source locator:** sec:construction
- **Numinous use:** Compare pairwise adjacency with actual higher faces.
- **Limit:** Small illustrative parameters do not inherit coboundary expansion automatically.
