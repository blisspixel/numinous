## What makes it hard

Point clouds do not certify Ahlfors-David regularity or uniform operator bounds at every truncation. Numerical smoothness is not rectifiability of a limit measure.

## Inside the construction

### geometric-diagnostic

Bilateral flatness controls both points' distance to a plane and holes in the plane's coverage; projection then controls pairwise metric distortion and target-ball coverage.

- **Source locator:** app:geometric-conventions
- **Numinous use:** A point-cloud inspector can show two directed distance errors and compare them with pairwise distortion under projection.
- **Limit:** Finite sampled distances are diagnostics for a chosen cloud, not a certificate of uniform rectifiability of an underlying measure.
