## The mathematical question

Degree-preserving graph switches allegedly mix polynomially for every graphical sequence and support exact uniform sampling.

## Visual and sonic possibility

Edges exchange partners in a fluid web; switch events are mapped percussion.

## What makes it hard

A finite chain run is approximate, not automatically the exact sampler. The bound 2n^8 is a theorem-scale guarantee and may be impractical; simple-graph constraints must reject loops and duplicate edges.

## Inside the construction

### construction

For two selected vertices, keep outside edges and each outside vertex's number of selected neighbors fixed; uniformly redistribute singleton neighbors as a fixed-size subset.

- **Source locator:** sec:setup
- **Numinous use:** Trade neighbors without changing any degree.
- **Limit:** The graph remains simple and the pair's own edge stays fixed.

### representation

Conditional expectations on these fibers are orthogonal projections; their complements sum to a PSD operator.

- **Source locator:** eq:pair-operators
- **Numinous use:** Compute the exact small-state transition and projection matrices.
- **Limit:** The global mixing inequality requires later sections.
