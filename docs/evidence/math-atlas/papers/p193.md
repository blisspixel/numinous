## Inside the construction

### representation

Insertion-deletion distance equals total lengths minus twice LCS; unit-cost edit distance differs by at most a factor two.

- **Source locator:** lem:alignments

- **Limit:** Substitution and insertion-deletion conventions must not be merged.

### measurement

Translation variation averages ell1 displacement over a finite abelian group; the lower-bound construction compares component shifts with simultaneous shifts.

- **Source locator:** eq:variation
