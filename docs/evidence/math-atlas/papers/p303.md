## Inside the construction

### construction

For primes congruent to two modulo three, (x,y) to (x cubed,y) permutes the affine plane and bends most deleted lines.

- **Source locator:** lem:gadget
- **Numinous use:** Compare survivors in the original and cubed coordinates.
- **Limit:** The survival theorem is for sufficiently large primes.

### invariant

The target cover budget charges a line one and a point one half; at cost at most s the asserted covers are full parallel classes.

- **Source locator:** con-weighted-budget
- **Numinous use:** Search exact mixed covers in finite examples.
- **Limit:** Small fields need not satisfy the probabilistic gadget theorem.
