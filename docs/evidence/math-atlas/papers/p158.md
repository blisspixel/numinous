## The mathematical question

Bochner-Riesz smoothing rounds a frequency-ball cutoff with an arbitrarily small positive order in the claimed three-dimensional bound.

## Visual and sonic possibility

Visual: linked source, spectrum, and evolving field views. Faithful playback only for an explicitly defined audible waveform; other norm-to-pitch mappings are sonification.

## What makes it hard

The strict positive-order result does not include the sharp zero-order ball multiplier. A periodic FFT approximation changes the ambient problem.

## Inside the construction

### computable-kernel

The three-dimensional radial filter has multiplier (1-|xi|^2)_+^delta and an explicit one-dimensional oscillatory integral for its radial kernel.

- **Source locator:** lem:br-kernel
- **Numinous use:** Link a frequency-ball taper control to a radial impulse-response view and finite convolution experiments.
- **Limit:** A sampled or periodic convolution is a discretization of the Euclidean operator and needs its own aliasing and quadrature limits.

### scale-accounting

The kernel is split into dyadic oscillatory shells; rescaling contributes a volume factor that changes the shell prefactor to lambda^(1-delta).

- **Source locator:** eq:br-rescaled-shell
- **Numinous use:** Show a shell's contribution and cumulative truncation error rather than only a final filtered image.
- **Limit:** The convergence estimate depends on the preceding oscillatory bound and cannot be established by adding a few rendered shells.
