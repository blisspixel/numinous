## Inside the construction

### algorithm

A support graph of degree b_R is greedily split into at most 2b_R-1 matchings, bounding the lifted block operator by that factor.

- **Source locator:** prop:transfer
- **Numinous use:** Turn matrix support into colored matchings and verify the resulting norm and propagation bounds.
- **Limit:** The simple greedy bound need not be optimal.

### approximation

Lazy Laplacian powers approximate the constant-fiber projection at rate (1-delta/2)^n. Sandwiching compact-entry operators gives local compactness; the amplified projection itself is not locally compact.

- **Source locator:** eq:gap; eq:sandwich
- **Numinous use:** Compare diffusion toward fiber averages with finite-propagation error certificates.
- **Limit:** A uniform spectral gap across all fibers is required for the infinite argument.
