## The mathematical question

Normal subgroups of rational points over function fields are claimed to be controlled by anisotropic local factors.

## What makes it hard

Finite normal closures cannot represent the topology or all abstract subgroups of rational points. Characteristic two is explicitly included in the claim. This is a finite group certificate, not a simulation of the number-field theorem. The characteristic-two application needs these exact hypotheses, not merely a group having many involutions.
