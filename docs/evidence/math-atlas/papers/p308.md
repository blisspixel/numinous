## The mathematical question

The pinned-distance claim says almost every point in a large planar set sees nearly linearly many distinct distances.

## Visual and sonic possibility

Distance circles bloom around the selected pin; shell radii may map to pitch.

## What makes it hard

All but o(n) is asymptotic and supplies no finite exceptional-point count here. Distinct points and exact distance equality matter, particularly in symmetric configurations or nearly coincident data.
