## The mathematical question

Finite tensor savings yield nonuniform sub-n-log-n Fourier circuits along an unbounded sequence of lengths.

## Visual and sonic possibility

Signal grids and transform paths animate one exact calculation; Fourier synthesis can provide genuine signal audio.

## What makes it hard

Selected lengths and free invertible monomial maps are part of this model. It is distinct from a uniform all-length fast numerical FFT, and preprocessing costs must not be hidden in practical comparisons.
