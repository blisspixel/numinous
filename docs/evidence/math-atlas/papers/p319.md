## What makes it hard

Separate embeddings need not agree on shared vertices, so their presence does not assemble a copy of the original graph. The decomposition must be fixed before sampling, not chosen after seeing the host.

## Inside the construction

### mechanism

A sufficiently high-probability family of Bernoulli edge sets can cover some target copy using a fixed number of members once r exceeds its expectation threshold.

- **Source locator:** cor:typical-cover
- **Numinous use:** Enumerate a tiny family and compare unions against target copies.
- **Limit:** The imported constant is 2^75, not a practical number of layers.

### invariant

Family monotonicity is unnecessary and repeated members in the covering tuple are allowed.

- **Source locator:** thm:discrete
- **Numinous use:** Test covers under these exact conventions.
- **Limit:** The selected step depends on an imported discrete-convexity theorem.
