Iterated matrix multiplication has claimed matching-scale depth-five homogeneous circuit bounds, linking matrix paths with algebraic complexity.

## Visual and sonic possibility

## What makes it hard

The lower bound is asymptotic and characteristic-zero. Track syntactic homogeneity explicitly; a layered drawing is insufficient, and gate bounds do not directly measure ordinary matrix-library runtime.

## Inside the construction

### construction

Replace variables in one group by derivatives and those in a disjoint group by multiplication, then restrict between specified homogeneous spaces.

- **Source locator:** eq:rank-map
- **Numinous use:** Build exact coefficient matrices for small polynomials.
- **Limit:** Formal derivatives behave differently in positive characteristic.

### invariant

The unprojected operator respects products, but the fixed-bidegree rank map does not; product arguments must first split bidegrees.

- **Source locator:** sec:rank
- **Numinous use:** Compare product operators with projected maps.
- **Limit:** Rank subadditivity is not rank multiplicativity.
