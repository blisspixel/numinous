## Inside the construction

### linear-algebra

A definite pair is normalized by T=(C-xA)/y so A wedge T=0 and T-squared=A-squared; A+iT determines an almost-complex structure up to sign.

- **Source locator:** eq:pair-normalization
- **Numinous use:** An exact four-dimensional exterior-algebra tool can compute Gram matrices and complex-plane tests.
- **Limit:** Pointwise algebra does not establish closedness or a global deformation.

### geometry

A third form completes a positive triple exactly when it tames one sign; simultaneous Lagrangian planes for the pair are complex lines.

- **Source locator:** lem:definite-pair
- **Numinous use:** Let users rotate coefficient vectors and inspect positivity cones and common planes.
- **Limit:** Individual Moser transports for different coefficients are not one simultaneous transport of the triple.
