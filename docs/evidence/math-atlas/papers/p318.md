## The mathematical question

The constructive companion claims a deterministic polynomial algorithm handling binary-encoded parallel-edge multiplicities.

## Visual and sonic possibility

Bundled host edges and sparse tree branches show compression; load-to-timbre is mapped.

## What makes it hard

Polynomial runtime does not guarantee useful constants. Expanding multiplicities into explicit edges destroys the intended encoding advantage; the one-vertex case needs separate trivial handling.

## Inside the construction

### algorithm

Polynomial-bit positive rational weights followed by whitening specify a frame with controlled norms and rational approximations to any requested precision.

- **Source locator:** lem:ta-frame
- **Numinous use:** Track precision separately from exact rank preservation.
- **Limit:** The displayed whitened vectors need not themselves be rational.

### identity

The log-determinant gradient is weighted effective resistance minus 1/q; its Hessian is a covariance of spanning-tree indicators.

- **Source locator:** eq:ta-gradient
- **Numinous use:** Compare optimization steps with exact small-tree probabilities.
- **Limit:** This normalization is one component of the full thin-tree algorithm.
