## Inside the construction

### exact-identity

A binary word's fourth Fourier moment equals N squared plus twice the sum of squared aperiodic autocorrelations, giving its exact merit factor for N at least two.

- **Source locator:** eq:merit-parseval
- **Numinous use:** A sign-flip game can connect visible sidelobes, an exact integer score and a waveform's Fourier profile.
- **Limit:** The asymptotic unbounded-merit claim gives no usable rate, and periodic autocorrelation would be a different convention.

### compositional-object

Finite words combine by (B times C)(s+b t)=B(s)C(t), producing reusable pattern blocks before any infinite Morse shift is formed.

- **Source locator:** cor:morse
- **Numinous use:** Let players compose signed motifs and inspect how the resulting finite word's correlations change.
- **Limit:** A finite prefix cannot establish unique ergodicity or the spectral type of an infinite dynamical system.
