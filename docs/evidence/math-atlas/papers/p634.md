## Inside the construction

### construction

A shared correction at each first-exit gate makes both signed norming tests inexpensive; its evaluations cancel when endpoint norms are averaged.

- **Source locator:** lem:paired
- **Numinous use:** Let users build head and tail tests and inspect correction cancellation.
- **Limit:** The correction relies on disjoint tree segments and ancestral heads.

### bound

The proof charges small crossings to an 8s-squared budget and pure tail terms to 4s-squared, producing sqrt(norm(x)^2+norm(y)^2/12).

- **Source locator:** eq:paired-parameter
- **Numinous use:** Display the actual segment cuts and a running energy budget.
- **Limit:** Finite branching cannot by itself invoke the countably branching diamond obstruction.
