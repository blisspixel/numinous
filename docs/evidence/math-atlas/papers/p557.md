## What makes it hard

Finite strategy search does not certify the unrestricted entangled value. This independence is under the original law before conditioning on tested wins. The optimal entangled value is a supremum over all finite dimensions; a bounded simulator only evaluates selected strategies.

## Inside the construction

### conditional-structure

Revealing Alice's questions before a cut and Bob's after it makes the remaining full local question strings conditionally independent, even when each original question pair is correlated.
