## The mathematical question

Sharp filling estimates extend to nonpositively curved metric spaces.

## Visual and sonic possibility

Proposed visual: Fill a closed triangulated surface with a three-dimensional mesh and vary the ambient metric. No physical sound model is claimed.

## What makes it hard

The theorem concerns integral cycles of dimension at least two; loop filling is only a prerequisite analogy, and a generic mesh is not automatically CAT(0).

## Inside the construction

### construction

The radial map moves x to fraction 1-tg(x) of the geodesic from a center; its swept current has boundary equal to the new cycle minus the old.

- **Source locator:** prop:radial
- **Numinous use:** A current editor can track orientation, multiplicity and swept volume under radial deformation.
- **Limit:** Cycle dimension is at least two in the theorem; loops are only prerequisite examples.

### certificate

CAT(0) triangle comparison yields an explicit two-point squared-distance bound feeding differential mass estimates.

- **Source locator:** eq:radial-two-point
- **Numinous use:** Compare geodesic interpolation and Euclidean comparison triangles, exporting distance inequalities.
- **Limit:** A sampled mesh does not establish global CAT(0) or sharp mass estimates.
