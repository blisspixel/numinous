## Inside the construction

### construction

Monomials multiply with a phase v raised to an alternating lattice pairing; elementary units are completed infinite products.

- **Source locator:** alg:torus; alg:units
- **Numinous use:** Reorder low-charge monomial products exactly.
- **Limit:** Noncommutativity must be retained.

### certificate

At fixed exponent and v-degree the admissibility conditions make coefficient sums finite; bounded output can be computed by rational linear algebra.

- **Source locator:** alg:reordering
- **Numinous use:** Show charge and degree cutoffs independently.
- **Limit:** The theorem's full positivity proof was not checked.
