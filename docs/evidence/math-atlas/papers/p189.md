## Inside the construction

### construction

Multiplication by a unitary 2 by 2 matrix preserves the real Gram matrix while altering mixed pairings.

- **Source locator:** lem:matching
- **Numinous use:** Rotate matrix factors without changing their Gram shape.
- **Limit:** The pairing matrix S may be indefinite, so it is not an inner product in general.

### frequency-control

A squared phase factor turns a cubic angular term into frequency one while keeping entries of degree at most two.

- **Source locator:** eq:rotation-pairings
- **Numinous use:** Display Fourier coefficients and algebraic cancellation under phase changes.
- **Limit:** The finite lift requires the later assembly, not only this matching identity.
