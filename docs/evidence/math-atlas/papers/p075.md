## The mathematical question

Sections on the whole reduced support of an adjoint are claimed to lift and force positive Iitaka dimension.

## Visual and sonic possibility

## What makes it hard

Componentwise semiampleness is not automatically semiampleness on the whole reduced support. The effective multiple and coefficient-one support are essential. Using the familiar left Euler formula without conversion reverses the sign. The finite operator model does not prove the filtered-module positivity theorem.
