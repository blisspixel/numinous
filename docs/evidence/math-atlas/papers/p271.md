## Visual and sonic possibility

## What makes it hard

Low-dimensional residual geometry illustrates conditioning, not the asymptotic theorem. Exact noiseless intersections need rank-aware numerical handling, and an apparent collapsed sphere may reflect precision loss.

## Inside the construction

### distribution

The squared projected length of a uniform direction onto an l-dimensional subspace has Beta(l/2,(D-l)/2) law.

- **Source locator:** s:coordinates
- **Numinous use:** Compare simulated projection lengths with the exact density.
- **Limit:** At dimensions zero or D the law is a point mass, not a beta law with zero shape.

### invariant

Conditional on the projection, the remaining direction stays uniform; the proof separates independent Gaussian lengths and directions.

- **Source locator:** s:coordinates
- **Numinous use:** Reveal coordinates while displaying the residual sphere.
- **Limit:** This geometry does not alone establish the memory lower bound.
