## What makes it hard

Finite tensor toys cannot establish Jiang-Su absorption. Finite stages illustrate the approximation, not central-sequence absorption. Commutation alone does not make arbitrary mixed tracial states independent.

### independence-boundary

Pairwise commuting row algebras are independent under extreme traces of the algebra they generate, including traces that need not extend to the ambient algebra.

- **Source locator:** lem:factor-independence
- **Numinous use:** Separate commutation, factorization of moments and choice of trace in a probability instrument.
- **Limit:** Commutation alone does not make arbitrary mixed tracial states independent.
