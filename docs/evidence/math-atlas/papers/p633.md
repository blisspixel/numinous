## What makes it hard

## Inside the construction

### combinatorics

Matched walks pair graph vertices with a fixed finite state set under compatibility and transition bounds; immediate reverse incidences are forbidden, including on loops.

- **Source locator:** lem:rare-walks
- **Numinous use:** A walk editor can distinguish edge instances, reverse incidences and allowed state matches.
- **Limit:** Parallel edges and loops cannot be simplified away.

### quantifier

The finite support is fixed before selecting a sufficiently large graph, and the proof counts rooted colored types rather than conditioning random colors on that support.

- **Source locator:** eq:bad-fraction
- **Numinous use:** Export rooted-type counts and rare-match bounds as finite combinatorial evidence.
- **Limit:** Every finite free space admits its identity as finite-rank; no finite block alone proves failure of BAP.
