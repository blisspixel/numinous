## What makes it hard

Finite diagonalization cannot establish a uniform many-particle gap; stability constants are existential. The finite Kraus identity is directly testable; uniform locality and gap stability require the separate analytic argument. Arbitrary damping does not inherit the manuscript's descent constants.
