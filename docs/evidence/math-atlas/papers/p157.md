## Inside the construction

### geometric-normalization

The construction matches a graph's value, gradient and positive definite Hessian to a reference phase by an explicit affine change using matrix square roots.

- **Source locator:** lem:continuation
- **Numinous use:** Build a local surface inspector that separates translation, tilt and curvature normalization.
- **Limit:** This is a local germ construction and requires a positive definite Hessian, not an arbitrary surface warp.

### controlled-gluing

A cutoff inserts the matched graph into a transition annulus; matching two jets makes the perturbation's Hessian small with the support scale.

- **Source locator:** eq:spherical-continuation; lem:continuation
- **Numinous use:** Let a player insert a small curved patch while inspecting derivatives and the remaining convexity margin.
- **Limit:** The allowed support size depends on derivative bounds; a visual seam test cannot certify global convexity or the extension theorem.
