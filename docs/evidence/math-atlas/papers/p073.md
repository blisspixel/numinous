## The mathematical question

Minimal semipositive metrics with zero Lelong numbers support a claimed interior cohomology injectivity theorem.

## Visual and sonic possibility

Visual: exact algebraic data and explicitly labelled prerequisite diagrams. Silence is appropriate; optional weight or degree sonification adds no evidence to the claim.

## What makes it hard

Finite-resolution smoothness cannot certify zero Lelong numbers everywhere. The geometric metric and cohomology hypotheses are not numerically decidable by this toy. Zero Lelong numbers and the integrability theorem are essential assumptions. Finite cochains do not establish the analytic primitive estimates or injectivity theorem.
