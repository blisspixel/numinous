## The mathematical question

Uniform permanence claims one compact trapping set eventually attracts every positive trajectory within a stoichiometric class.

## Visual and sonic possibility

Concentration streams and phase-space enclosures show chemistry; concentration-to-tone is mapped.

## What makes it hard

Weak reversibility and constant positive rates are essential. A observed enclosure is not the claimed uniform trapping set, and different initial states must belong to the same compatibility class.
