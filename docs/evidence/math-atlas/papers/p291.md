## The mathematical question

Triple averages with arbitrary distinct nonzero integer slopes include negative-time observation of invertible mixing systems.

## Visual and sonic possibility

Counter-moving traces create an immediate instrument; sampled observables can be audified.

## What makes it hard

Negative slopes require a genuine inverse, not reversed numerical integration guessed from a dissipative map. Distinct nonzero slopes are essential; repeated slopes produce a different limit expression.

## Inside the construction

### construction

A local kernel integrates four inputs along x+b_j t, stays away from interval boundaries, and has zero marginal in every slope direction.

- **Source locator:** def:kernels
- **Numinous use:** Plot support strips and test directional cancellation.
- **Limit:** Constants may depend on the fixed slopes.

### invariant

Dividing slopes by their common gcd changes the base transformation to T^g without changing sampled iterates.

- **Source locator:** sec:local
- **Numinous use:** Normalize slope tuples exactly.
- **Limit:** No uniform estimate over all integer slope choices is asserted.
