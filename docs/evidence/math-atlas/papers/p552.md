## The mathematical question

Product projections limit shallow quantum circuits' ability to compute parity.

## Visual and sonic possibility

Proposed visual: Build a tiny parity circuit and watch correlations spread by layer. No physical sound model is claimed.

## What makes it hard

Small circuit failure does not prove the asymptotic lower bound. Primitive fanout, intermediate measurements and postselection are excluded from the model. A product projection may have high global rank; finite leakage does not prove the asymptotic lower bound.
