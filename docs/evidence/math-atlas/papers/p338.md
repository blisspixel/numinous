## The mathematical question

Random triangle removal allegedly leaves a sharp normalized edge count approaching one over twice square-root two.

## Visual and sonic possibility

Triangles dissolve into a residual web; removals can drive mapped percussion.

## What makes it hard

Selection must be uniform among remaining triangles, not among vertices or edges. The limit constant is asymptotic and L2 convergence gives no immediate finite-size error bar.

## Inside the construction

### algorithm

Assign independent continuous priorities to all initial triangles and greedily delete an intact triangle when its turn arrives.

- **Source locator:** lem:priority
- **Numinous use:** Compare priority-scan and stepwise random-removal runs.
- **Limit:** Rejected triangles never become present again.

### invariant

Conditional on any scanned prefix, the first remaining intact triangle is uniform among currently present triangles.

- **Source locator:** lem:priority
- **Numinous use:** Verify exact transition probabilities on a tiny graph.
- **Limit:** Fresh randomness for recursive unfoldings is an auxiliary model, not the same coupled process.
