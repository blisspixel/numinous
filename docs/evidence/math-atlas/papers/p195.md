## Inside the construction

### construction

A prescribed length M admits binary codewords of width M^2, producing encoded words of length M^3 with a stated absolute distortion bound.

- **Source locator:** thm:804
- **Numinous use:** Build and test binary substitutions for a fixed small alphabet and length.
- **Limit:** The result is not one universal code for every word length.

### certificate

Long segments of unequal codewords have LCS at most 0.99 times their length; a union bound over segment pairs supplies existence.

- **Source locator:** lem:random-segments
- **Numinous use:** Search candidate codes and export exhaustive segment-LCS certificates.
- **Limit:** The asymptotic threshold may make small examples fail the stated constants.
