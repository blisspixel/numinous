## The mathematical question

Polar products are claimed to contain symplectic balls of every capacity below four, linking convex geometry and phase space.

## Visual and sonic possibility

Visual: coupled geometric objects with exact coordinates and invariant readings. Optional invariant-to-pitch sonification is a chosen mapping, not inherent geometric sound.

## What makes it hard

Volume preservation alone is not symplecticity. A two-dimensional animation cannot establish embeddings into all higher-dimensional polar products.

## Inside the construction

### explicit-map

The planar map is an odd power series with coefficients 8/pi^2 times alternating reciprocal odd squares; its logarithmic derivative is 8/pi^2 arctan(w).

- **Source locator:** eq:planar-F; eq:planar-arctan
- **Numinous use:** Deform a colored complex grid into the auxiliary lens while comparing a truncated series with its derivative.
- **Limit:** Convergence and branch choices must be explicit; this planar map is not the whole higher-dimensional symplectic construction.

### inverse-mechanism

On a fixed horizontal image slice, the radius derivative of the real coordinate is positive through a squared derivative divided by r Re(wF').

- **Source locator:** eq:horizontal-radius-derivative; eq:planar-T
- **Numinous use:** Provide bracketed slice inversion and let players see conditioning deteriorate near the boundary.
- **Limit:** The boundary derivative can diverge; a uniform slider step is not a uniform inverse-error bound.
