## Inside the construction

### representation

Walk labels belong to oriented edge instances, reverse sign under reversal, and need not be differences of a potential on states.

- **Source locator:** lem:edge-labels
- **Numinous use:** A reversible-walk instrument can accumulate edge vectors while checking detailed balance.
- **Limit:** Collapsing parallel edges or forcing a state potential changes the data.

### construction

Finite charts retain landmarks along words of bounded length; good transitions follow order maps, producing a positive probability of linear displacement with unit edge labels.

- **Source locator:** sec:walks; prop:walks
- **Numinous use:** Visualize chart transport and compare displacement moments across time.
- **Limit:** State counts can grow rapidly with word length; finite experiments do not establish superreflexivity.
