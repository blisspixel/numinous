## What makes it hard

The statement concerns images of complex linear representations, not the entire abstract fundamental group. Open varieties differ from the compact abelianity case. Arbitrary representation quotients need not inherit the mixed Hodge structures used by the proof. A rational Lie model can erase finite torsion and therefore cannot recover this distinction by itself.
