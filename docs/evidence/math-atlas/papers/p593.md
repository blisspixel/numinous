## What makes it hard

Matching drawn cones does not certify isomorphism of the target algebras. Finite cone pictures are prerequisite examples and do not classify arbitrary stabilized algebras. Rational UHF fractions change ranks, not amplitudes; scaling all entries down is an incorrect finite analogy.

## Inside the construction

### invariant-boundary

Zero/infinity ideal weights encode ideal inclusion by tau_I+tau_J=tau_I; restricting the invariant to bounded normalized traces would discard this information.

- **Source locator:** intro:ideal-weight
- **Numinous use:** Provide an extended-cone and ideal-poset viewer with explicit infinity arithmetic.

### error-mechanism

Small stabilizers are small in relative ranks rather than operator norm, while fixed positive cutoffs retain lower comparison witnesses as approximation stages change.

- **Source locator:** sm:stabilizers; sm:fixed-lower-cut; sm:witness-bound
