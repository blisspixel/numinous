## The mathematical question

Mean-payoff parity games require sustained reward and a recurring-priority condition, creating conflict between gain and admissible infinite behavior.

## Visual and sonic possibility

Loops display reward balances and priority colors; tones may encode priorities through an explicit mapping.

## What makes it hard

Finite visits cannot establish conditions on infinitely recurring priorities. Display certified recurrent strategy structure separately from animated play and retain signed binary rewards without floating-point threshold mistakes.

## Inside the construction

### invariant

Intermediate labels guarantee containment only for dominions below supplied size bounds; only the full-size call identifies winning regions.

- **Source locator:** eq:label-guarantee
- **Numinous use:** Show provisional labels with their current certified scope.
- **Limit:** A provisional label is not a winning-strategy certificate.

### construction

The recursion halves the opposing player's size bound and removes the current minimum-priority attractor to form a residual arena.

- **Source locator:** eq:current-residual
- **Numinous use:** Animate attractor growth and the shrinking recursive arena.
