## The mathematical question

The claimed exact DFT algorithm saves a tiny power of logarithm under unrestricted exact complex arithmetic.

## Visual and sonic possibility

Epicycles and harmonic spectra show the actual transform; synthesized Fourier audio is intrinsic to the signal.

## What makes it hard

The saving exponent is 10^-13 and the model is not floating-point bit complexity. This supplies no evidence of practical FFT acceleration or improved audio performance without separate implementation and numerical analysis.

## Inside the construction

### construction

The local compiler uses exactly r coordinates, alternating monomial layers and disjoint copies of a fixed two-coordinate operation.
