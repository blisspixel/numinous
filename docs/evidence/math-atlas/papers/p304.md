## The mathematical question

The finite sums-and-products theorem claims a shared monochromatic color for all nonempty subset sums and products of a finite set.

## Visual and sonic possibility

Sum and product trees share colored leaves; resulting integers may map to rhythm.

## What makes it hard

A finite coloring window cannot represent every positive integer, and witness search may leave the window. Duplicate arithmetic values need set semantics; a failed bounded search says nothing against the infinite theorem.

## Inside the construction

### distribution

- **Source locator:** lem:sampling
