## Inside the construction

### proof-mechanism

Endpoint maps are not uniformly close across components. Orthogonal copies at every height cancel their common interiors after shifting one level.

- **Source locator:** ana:endpoints
- **Numinous use:** Animate operator stacks and their cancellation while displaying propagation and operator norm.
- **Limit:** A finite stack is an analogy; the assembly kernel uses an infinite graph union.

### certificate

A doubled-module rotation has propagation at most R+1, uniformly in stack length; multiplicativity defects vanish modulo compact operators.

- **Source locator:** eq:stack-rotation; eq:compression-defect
- **Numinous use:** Supply sparse block matrices with explicit propagation envelopes and rotation residuals.
- **Limit:** Concrete norm calculations do not establish a maximal-completion statement.
