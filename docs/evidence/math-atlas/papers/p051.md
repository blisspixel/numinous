## What makes it hard

The introduction leaves denominators unrestricted; optimizing term count can create impractically enormous denominators. The theorem does not imply every fraction needs that many terms. Equal numerical prime values do not identify distinct sampled variables. The numerical exponential saving is an asymptotic claim with large-parameter hypotheses, not a small-example guarantee.
