## The mathematical question

A degree-d polynomial threshold on a Boolean cube allegedly has average sensitivity bounded by 8d sqrt(n).

## Visual and sonic possibility

Boundary edges glow on cube projections; flips may trigger mapped percussion.

## What makes it hard

Use the declared sign convention at zero. Small-cube measurements are exact experiments, not validation of the all-dimensional bound, and coefficient cancellation can lower actual polynomial degree.

## Inside the construction

### representation

Multiplication by sqrt(w) transports weighted cube functions into the counting inner product, preserving a point basis for edge accounting.

- **Source locator:** sec:grading
- **Numinous use:** Change vertex weights and watch the polynomial subspaces tilt.
- **Limit:** Weights must remain strictly positive.

### construction

Orthogonal degree layers are K_k intersect orthogonal-complement(K_(k-1)), where K_k is the weighted image of degree-at-most-k characters.

- **Source locator:** sec:grading
- **Numinous use:** Build layers by exact small-cube orthogonalization.
