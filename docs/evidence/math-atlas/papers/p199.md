## What makes it hard

## Inside the construction

### error-bound

Freezing direction on an interval moves the radial coordinate by order mesh width but produces transverse error of order width squared.

- **Source locator:** eq:transverse-error
- **Numinous use:** Display exact sweep points against a frozen-direction approximation and its error band.
- **Limit:** The derivative hypothesis is required for the second-order cancellation.

### construction

An enlarged triangle includes a negative radial collar as well as transverse error, and perpendicular projection remains nonsingular.

- **Source locator:** eq:triangle
- **Numinous use:** Construct a certified triangular footprint for each interval and refine interactively.
- **Limit:** The negative collar cannot be clipped away without rechecking containment.
