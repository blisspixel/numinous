## The mathematical question

The sampler claims subpolynomial first-order queries for strongly conditioned log-concave targets, allowing unrestricted internal computation.

## Visual and sonic possibility

## What makes it hard

Few queries do not mean little runtime. Hessian bounds between identity and twice identity, a known minimizer, and fixed total-variation error are essential; this is not exact sampling.
