## Inside the construction

### algorithm

Compute A and B as child ell2 norms, then P=max(A,v+B), Q=max(B,-v+A); P-Q=v and at least one child constraint is tight.

- **Source locator:** rec:definition
- **Numinous use:** Provide a linear-time finite-tree norm evaluator with both fields visible.
- **Limit:** Root-sum and zero-root common-field norms differ by a factor two on the shared subspace.

### certificate

The energy [d(2P+d)(2Q+d)]^(2/3) dominates the sum of child energies, avoiding a loss factor at each depth.

- **Source locator:** rec:two-field
- **Numinous use:** Show a local energy certificate at every node and export the full tree calculation.
- **Limit:** The cubic asymptotic modulus requires the finite-head and completion hypotheses.
