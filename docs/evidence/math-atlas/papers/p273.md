## Inside the construction

### construction

Arithmetic in k[T]/F runs a field procedure on all unknown roots simultaneously when F is square-free and split.

- **Source locator:** ff:product

- **Limit:** The split and square-free hypotheses are essential.

### certificate

A zero test computes gcd(F,a): either all components agree or a proper factor certifies their disagreement.

- **Source locator:** ff:simultaneous
- **Numinous use:** Turn a divergent branch into an explicit factor witness.
- **Limit:** This local tool does not establish the manuscript's very large polynomial runtime bound.
