## The mathematical question

The Heilbronn construction claims point sets whose smallest triangle area beats the near-n^-2 scale by a tiny fixed exponent.

## Visual and sonic possibility

A changing web of thinnest triangles is visually immediate; minimum-area tones are mapped.

## What makes it hard

The exponent is extremely small and asymptotic, so there is no implied practical packing advantage. Floating-point collinearity needs exact predicates, and local optimization gives only a construction lower bound.

## Inside the construction

### construction

Multiply all Frobenius conjugates of a three-column determinant over an extension field to obtain a polynomial over the prime field.

- **Source locator:** norm:polynomial
- **Numinous use:** Compare determinant values and their field norms.
- **Limit:** The manuscript fixes extension degree 41 and enormous subsequent parameters.

### invariant

The norm is nonzero exactly when the extension-field determinant is nonzero, providing an arithmetic obstruction to small determinant representatives.

- **Source locator:** norm:section
- **Numinous use:** Use smaller fields to inspect the nonvanishing identity.
- **Limit:** A smaller illustrative degree does not inherit the triangle-area improvement.
