## Inside the construction

### representation

The 2^n layers are indexed by subsets I, with shift minus sum(2i-1) and localization height n-max(I); the empty subset supplies the canonical unit.

- **Source locator:** eq:pieces
- **Numinous use:** Generate the layer list exactly and allow users to reorder compatible blocks.
- **Limit:** A list of layers does not imply a wedge decomposition.

### proof-mechanism

The same product maps must be bases at every lower-height test, allowing successive cofibers to remove the highest remaining layer.

- **Source locator:** sec:assembly
- **Numinous use:** Represent maps and cofibers as first-class data, with compatibility checks.
- **Limit:** The excerpt establishes architecture and conventions, not a practical map-computation algorithm.
