## Inside the construction

### operator

Changing measure to e^V m leaves weak gradients unchanged but adds the drift Gamma(V,u) to the Laplacian.

- **Source locator:** weight:generator

- **Limit:** The identity uses the stated bounded Lipschitz and RCD hypotheses.

### mechanism

A weak Hessian upper bound for F changes the curvature-dimension lower function to K-lambda G under the weight e^(lambda F), while the metric stays fixed.

- **Source locator:** weight:variable-cd
- **Numinous use:** Display geometry, measure and curvature bound as separate editable quantities.
- **Limit:** This is an analytic implication, not a certificate from finite differences.
