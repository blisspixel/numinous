## The mathematical question

Close C-star algebras can resist conjugacy despite related weak closures.

## Visual and sonic possibility

Proposed visual: Show several notions of closure in a carefully labeled conceptual diagram. No physical sound model is claimed.

## What makes it hard

Finite-dimensional closures coincide and cannot realize the target distinction. Any finite prefix alone hides the condition responsible for the obstruction. The statement is failure of spatial unitary conjugacy for close C*-algebras with the same bicommutant, not abstract nonisomorphism or a von Neumann algebra counterexample.
