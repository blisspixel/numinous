## The mathematical question

Planar shortest-path metrics are claimed to embed in L1 with universal distortion, suggesting weighted cuts as a usable representation.

## Visual and sonic possibility

Visual: editable graph, highlighted paths, and exact distance tables. Route-event rhythm is optional sonification; graph distance retains its declared mathematical units.

## What makes it hard

A two-dimensional point layout is not a general L1 embedding. Existence of a universal constant does not specify an efficient optimal construction.

## Inside the construction

### cut-metric

For threshold cuts with two signs, symmetric-difference measure is max(abs(delta h), kappa abs(delta P)).

- **Source locator:** sets:formula; sets:distance
- **Numinous use:** Pair two sliders with the moving cut sets and the exact induced distance.
- **Limit:** The measure normalization and shared labels are essential; independently relabeling cuts changes the metric.

### coordinate-change

A monotone vertical reparametrization can absorb a branch function while retaining the very same cut sets; a fresh independent sign supports the next stage.

- **Source locator:** sets:vertical
- **Numinous use:** Make a chart-change action that moves coordinates but preserves set identities and measured distances.
- **Limit:** This local mechanism is only part of the planar-graph embedding construction; a finite demo does not establish its global distortion bound.
