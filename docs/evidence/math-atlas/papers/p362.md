## What makes it hard

## Inside the construction

### construction

A staircase band is represented by row and column blocks of size at most two and a row-tensor-column generator in its group-algebra module.

- **Source locator:** band:sets; band:generator
- **Numinous use:** Let users assemble the band and inspect the signed row/column generator.
- **Limit:** The generator must be retained; arbitrary submodules with similar dimensions are not interchangeable.

### certificate

When a group permutes a direct sum of sectors transitively, coset translates of a cyclic stabilizer module lie in disjoint sectors, yielding the full induced-module isomorphism.

- **Source locator:** lem:sector-induction
- **Numinous use:** Animate coset copies occupying separate coordinate sectors and verify rank additivity.
- **Limit:** The direct-sum sector hypotheses are essential to injectivity.
