## What makes it hard

Negative curvature pictures do not establish Brody hyperbolicity in general, and a curve example does not verify the higher-dimensional claim. Numerical frame fitting is not a proof that the required holomorphic frame exists. The Sobolev regularity and extremal-disc hypotheses are essential to the analytic theorem.

## Inside the construction

### selected-proof-mechanism

A boundary-unitary holomorphic frame makes log determinant zero on the boundary; its interior Laplacian becomes a Ricci term because the frame determinant contributes a harmonic function.
