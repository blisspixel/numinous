## The mathematical question

Bruhat-interval isomorphisms allegedly preserve equal-parameter Kazhdan-Lusztig polynomials in arbitrary Coxeter systems.

## Visual and sonic possibility

Hasse diagrams and coefficient bars provide the visual; coefficients map to notes.

## What makes it hard

The equal-parameter hypothesis is essential. A graph-layout similarity is not a poset isomorphism, and arbitrary posets need not be Bruhat intervals; exact combinatorial data must drive every comparison.
