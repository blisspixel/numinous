## What makes it hard

Finite cohomology does not establish bounded infinite-operator cohomology. Finite-dimensional calculations do not verify bounded primitives in arbitrary von Neumann algebras. The theorem is subsequential and in probability; a finite simulation neither realizes exact freeness nor verifies cohomology vanishing.
