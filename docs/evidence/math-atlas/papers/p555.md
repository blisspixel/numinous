## Inside the construction

### constructive-transfer

An explicit continuity-equation flow sends uniform density to a desired positive charge density. Transporting eight equal-weight Gauss nodes per cube preserves their equal masses.

- **Source locator:** subsec:charge-placement
- **Numinous use:** A density-to-particles object lets players sculpt a field and watch equal-charge beads follow the transport.
- **Limit:** Polynomial exactness is in original cube coordinates on integrands composed with the flow.

### error-separation

The construction distinguishes an all-state form error of order h squared from a localized-mode compression error of order h to the fourth; a complementary gap makes coupling errors quadratic.

- **Source locator:** prop:nuclear-replacement
- **Numinous use:** Attach two explicit error readings to continuous-to-discrete potential approximations.
- **Limit:** These estimates do not reproduce the full hardness reduction or make continuum chemistry easy.
