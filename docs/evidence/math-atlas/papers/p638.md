## Inside the construction

### optimization

The norm is the least cost norm2(h)+norm1(mu) subject to x=h+B mu, where B sums root-path vectors.

- **Source locator:** prop:linear-duality
- **Numinous use:** Implement a finite convex decomposition tool returning both path coefficients and residual.
- **Limit:** Replacing the sum by a Euclidean combined cost defines a different norm.

### certificate

The dual norm is max(norm2(f),maximum absolute root-prefix sum), yielding a concrete primal-dual gap.

- **Source locator:** eq:linear-polar
- **Numinous use:** Let players clip path potentials while experts obtain a bound certificate.
- **Limit:** Infinite-component norm equivalence and asymptotic conclusions require the stated completion arguments.
