## The mathematical question

Two-way nondeterministic complementation allegedly needs exponentially more states when alphabet size may grow.

## Visual and sonic possibility

Heads traverse symbol tapes while branch paths light; transitions produce optional mapped clicks.

## What makes it hard

Finite test words do not certify language equivalence. The alphabet grows in the construction, so never present the lower bound as an unchanged fixed-alphabet result or conflate nondeterminism with random probability.
