## The mathematical question

Largest-prime-factor scales for neighboring integers are claimed to become independent with Dickman marginals.

## Visual and sonic possibility

## What makes it hard

Consecutive integers are arithmetically dependent even when these normalized observables have an independent limit. Finite histograms do not establish that limit. The manuscript proves existence through sampling, not a universally efficient exact cut-norm algorithm. An integrated moment bound is not a uniform conditional bound for every row type, and finite success does not verify the joint Dickman law.
