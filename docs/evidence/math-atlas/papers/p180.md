## The mathematical question

A radial Fourier certificate is claimed to attain the optimal planar packing bound through global sign conditions.

## Visual and sonic possibility

Visual: shared particles, Voronoi cells, reciprocal-space peaks, and energy readings. Energy or defect tones are sonification, not a physical sound prediction.

## What makes it hard

Sampling positive Fourier values is not a global sign proof. Periodic equality uniqueness differs from classification of every nonperiodic packing.

## Inside the construction

### certificate-arithmetic

The small matrix block can be enclosed by rational cosine quadrature with positive weights summing to one and a separate analytic Taylor remainder.

- **Source locator:** cert:rational-small-error; cert:rational-small-quadrature; cert:rational-small-weight-total
- **Numinous use:** Offer a Rust certificate replay that shows every matrix-entry interval and its quadrature and rounding contributions.
- **Limit:** The proposed small-block check does not certify the whole infinite-dimensional construction; the manuscript's reported intervals still require independent replay.

### numerical-boundary

Exact target periodicity can reduce an angle before approximating pi, but replacing pi by a rational approximation does not make the approximating exponential exactly periodic.

- **Source locator:** cert:rational-small
- **Numinous use:** Expose argument reduction and transcendental approximation as separate steps in a reproducible arithmetic trace.
- **Limit:** Treating an approximate constant as exact in a periodicity identity can invalidate a claimed enclosure.
