## The mathematical question

The finite-field group-ring counterexample also allegedly yields an injective but nonsurjective cellular automaton on an infinite group.

## What makes it hard

## Inside the construction

### representation

The right-neighbor rule (T_c x)(g)=sum c_u x(gu) satisfies T_c T_d=T_cd; the delta configuration reads individual coefficients and makes this representation faithful.

- **Source locator:** lem:ca-representation
- **Numinous use:** Provide a local convolution editor with explicit multiplication order and delta probes.
- **Limit:** A finite display is only a window into configurations on the full group.

### certificate

When ab=1, T_b is injective and its image consists exactly of configurations fixed by T_ba; a nonzero coefficient of ba-1 supplies a delta configuration outside the image.

- **Source locator:** prop:ca-transfer
- **Numinous use:** Paint a delta pattern and display the local image equation that rejects it.
- **Limit:** An injective nonsurjective self-map cannot be reproduced on a finite configuration space.
