## The mathematical question

A trilinear singular transform with fixed slopes one, two, and three is claimed to map three L3 inputs into L1.

## Visual and sonic possibility

Visual: linked source, spectrum, and evolving field views. Faithful playback only for an explicitly defined audible waveform; other norm-to-pitch mappings are sonification.

## What makes it hard

This paper fixes the slopes; arbitrary slope controls would leave its stated theorem. Finite principal-value quadrature requires cancellation-aware error checks.

## Inside the construction

### localization-mechanism

A local four-input form samples x+jt for j=0,1,2,3, with cancellation along each input coordinate and declared support/derivative conditions.

- **Limit:** A generic kernel or a toy finite sum need not satisfy the admissibility conditions used by the theorem.

### tree-budget

- **Limit:** This reduction assumes the normalized absorption estimate. Building the stopping tree alone does not establish its difficult uniform constant.
