## The mathematical question

The weak-mixing triangle manuscript claims every triangle with an irrational angle has ergodic product flow.

## Visual and sonic possibility

Two ray families weave across the triangle; collisions may map to rhythmic voices.

## What makes it hard

Weak mixing is stronger than single-flow ergodicity and supplies no rate. Floating-point angles cannot certify irrationality relative to pi, and finite paths do not establish the theorem.

## Inside the construction

### construction

Glue oppositely oriented triangle copies along sides so reflection becomes straight continuation, with cone angles twice the triangle angles.
