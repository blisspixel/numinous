## Inside the construction

### representation

The exterior conformal map defines Faber polynomials through q/(h-z); its comparison kernel is positive with both angular marginals one, without requiring symmetry.

- **Source locator:** lem:faber
- **Numinous use:** Compute boundary kernels and polynomial coefficients for disks and ellipses.
- **Limit:** General conformal maps require certified approximation; a sampled kernel is not a proof.

### energy

Positive and negative Fourier parts separate, and the kernel contracts L2 by positivity and its two marginals, yielding a coefficient-energy estimate.

- **Source locator:** prop:coefficients
- **Numinous use:** Compare polynomial matrix filters, boundary norms and Fourier energy.
- **Limit:** Coefficient matrices need not commute; evaluation order must be explicit.
