### identity

- **Numinous use:** A scalar profile instrument can expose the gap and its cancellation under interval averaging.

### geometry

The reference reciprocal sine-square profile has an explicit chord integral, enabling exact normalization before determinant comparison.

- **Source locator:** weighted:chord
