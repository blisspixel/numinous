## The mathematical question

Correspondence coloring extends local palettes by allowing edge-specific matchings of incompatible colors, with clique-free logarithmic bounds.

## Visual and sonic possibility

Twisted color ribbons expose the generalization; conflicts can map to sound.

## What makes it hard

The asymptotic degree threshold and constants depend on the excluded graph. Ordinary/list coloring are special cases, not equivalent data models; preserve edge-specific color correspondences in every export.
