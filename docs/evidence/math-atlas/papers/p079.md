## The mathematical question

Low-dimensional log Calabi-Yau fibrations are claimed to have uniformly bounded moduli denominators and effective base systems.

## Visual and sonic possibility

Visual: exact algebraic data and explicitly labelled prerequisite diagrams. Silence is appropriate; optional weight or degree sonification adds no evidence to the claim.

## What makes it hard

A denominator least common multiple alone does not certify a b-Cartier bound. Positive-dimensional base and dimension-at-most-four restrictions matter. A class becoming torsion does not alone clear the original rational coefficients. This requires supplied resolution and positivity data; the tool would not produce a good model automatically.
