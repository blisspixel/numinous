## The mathematical question

The torsion-free characteristic-two construction allegedly has a one-sided group-algebra inverse that is not two-sided.

## Visual and sonic possibility

## What makes it hard

Finite square matrices cannot reproduce this phenomenon over a field. A finite periodic automaton surrogate would erase the essential infinite-group behavior; boundary truncations must never count as witnesses.
