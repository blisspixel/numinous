## Inside the construction

### certificate

Adding r two-cells and r three-cells gives a cellular projection q with q d3=I; this proves the required cohomological vanishing for every coefficient module.

- **Source locator:** eq:split-boundary
- **Numinous use:** An exact chain-complex editor can verify the splitting identity and relative acyclic pair.
- **Limit:** Algebraic splitting does not automatically remove the three-cells geometrically.

### obstruction

For finite two-complexes, vanishing twisted H2 forces a character to be trivial on torsion; this detects failure of a two-dimensional model.

- **Source locator:** eq:obstruction-overview
- **Numinous use:** Compare twisted boundary matrices under chosen one-dimensional characters.
- **Limit:** The constructed example has infinite fundamental group; finite matrices need specified representations.
