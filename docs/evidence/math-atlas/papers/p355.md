## Inside the construction

### certificate

The coefficient algebra is a division algebra, but the selected homogeneous forms are required to commute pairwise; this is a separate, testable hypothesis.

- **Source locator:** lem:ordered-basis
- **Numinous use:** Show commutator checks before enabling a triangular-reduction certificate.
- **Limit:** Generic matrices do not constitute a division algebra.

### representation
