## Inside the construction

### construction

A periodic planar processor becomes autonomous by adding a third-coordinate clock equal to one on its corridor, with compensating behavior elsewhere to keep mean zero.

- **Source locator:** eq:autonomous-suspension
- **Numinous use:** Let users compare a timed planar program and its spatial clock suspension.
- **Limit:** The clock need not be positive everywhere; correctness is confined to the tracked corridor.

### clock

A nonnegative rate preserves all finite computational times only if its integral diverges; the force includes rate derivative, squared-rate advection and viscous terms.

- **Source locator:** prop:scalar-clock
- **Numinous use:** Offer a clock editor displaying accumulated time and the resulting force components.
- **Limit:** A clock with finite total time may stop before a future event.

### gauge

Solenoidal projection changes pressure while preserving velocity and the tracked event.

- **Source locator:** lem:projection
- **Numinous use:** Compare force gauges on the same motion.
- **Limit:** This is prescribed forcing, not an arbitrary unforced-flow universality claim.
