## The mathematical question

Polar duality does not preserve the proposed covering-entropy relation universally.

## Visual and sonic possibility

Proposed visual: Cover a convex body with small copies, then flip to its polar. No physical sound model is claimed.

## What makes it hard

Numerical covers need certified containment; the counterexample may live in high dimension.

## Inside the construction

### construction

Separated rows produce K=3 absconv(rows)+t cube, while a short uniform approximation list for the column hull bounds a polar covering number.

- **Source locator:** lem:matrix-to-bodies
- **Numinous use:** Turn a matrix into primal and polar bodies, retaining row separation and cover witnesses.
- **Limit:** The small cube is necessary for full-dimensional interior.

### certificate

The support function is exactly 3 max_x abs(row_x dot delta)+t norm1(delta), giving computable polar membership.

- **Source locator:** eq:row-support-function
- **Numinous use:** Evaluate support and polarity exactly and compare certified packing lower bounds with covering upper bounds.
- **Limit:** A heuristic cover count must not be labeled the minimum; arbitrary ambient centers are allowed.
