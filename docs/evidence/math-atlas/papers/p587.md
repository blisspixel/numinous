## The mathematical question

A finite factor can reportedly be encoded by one generator.

## Visual and sonic possibility

Proposed visual: Combine several small matrices into an encoded operator and try to recover them. No physical sound model is claimed.

## What makes it hard

A finite example does not prove single generation for infinite factors. Finite word truncation yields an approximation diagnostic, not a certificate of weak generation in a II1 factor. Dense G-delta existence is not an efficient algorithm for finding generators and does not imply continuous generators over bundles.

## Inside the construction

### diagnostic-construction

Distance from a target vector to the generated L2 subspace is an infimum over finite noncommutative polynomials; fixed polynomial evaluations vary continuously in the trace 2-norm.

- **Source locator:** lem:projection-continuity

### topology-boundary

The genericity argument uses completeness of the unitary group in trace 2-norm and simultaneous continuity points of projection norms.

- **Source locator:** lem:unitary-completeness; lem:projection-continuity
