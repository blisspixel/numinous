## The mathematical question

Integer polymatroid common bases count each capacity vector once, avoiding expansion into distinguishable labeled copies.

## Visual and sonic possibility

Allocation bars and active subset limits reveal constraints; capacity changes may map to notes.

## What makes it hard

Oracle calls carry real computational cost. Expanding capacity into labeled tokens changes the counting measure, and a generic feasible-allocation sampler does not inherit the manuscript's approximation contract.

## Inside the construction

### certificate

Weights evaluated after two coordinate additions or removals form matrices with at most one positive eigenvalue, including diagonal double moves.

- **Source locator:** def:integer-signatures
- **Numinous use:** Compute inertia for finite weighted integer slices.
- **Limit:** Invalid moves have zero weight and absent tests are vacuous.

### mechanism

A one-positive-direction form is nonpositive on the appropriate orthogonal hyperplane, yielding a quadratic projection inequality.

- **Source locator:** eq:signature-projection
- **Numinous use:** Move vectors through the positive direction and its complement.
- **Limit:** Numerical eigenvalues near zero require exact or interval certification.
