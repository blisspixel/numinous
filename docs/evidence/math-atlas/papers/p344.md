## The mathematical question

Lech's conjecture claims Hilbert-Samuel multiplicity cannot decrease under flat local maps in arbitrary dimensions and characteristics.

## Visual and sonic possibility

Monomial staircases and growth curves show multiplicity; coefficient-to-tone is mapped.

## What makes it hard

Finite growth fits do not certify eventual multiplicity, and arbitrary deformations are not flat. The broad theorem needs algebraic hypotheses unavailable from pictures alone; use exact supported families.

## Inside the construction

### invariant

The argument assumes parameter-power quotient length scales as a^h and positive Koszul homology has length zero.
